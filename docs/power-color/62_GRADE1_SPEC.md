# Power Color — Grade 1 Content Spec

Grade 1 extends Power Color on the existing shared Power TOEIC question-bank/workout/session engine. It does not fork a Grade 1 engine.

## Scope authority
The current AFT Grade 1 official scope, official textbook table of contents, official sample questions, and current examination notices define the Grade 1 boundary. Public AFT headings establish topic membership/order, but unpublished textbook body text must not be reconstructed by inference.

## Source contract
For each official subheading, audit before authoring. Classify `SOURCE_RESOLVED`, `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`, or `SOURCE_BLOCKED`.

A fact may be authored when it is deterministic and auditable from AFT public material or from a strong corroborating authority appropriate to the claim (museum/scientific conservation record, government, standards body, university, academic society, or original historical/scientific source). Corroboration does not prove that an arbitrary fact appears in the unpublished AFT text; therefore keep questions narrowly aligned to the official heading and do not claim exhaustive textbook coverage.

## Question contract
- pending-first
- four distinct options where the shared schema uses four-choice questions
- independently solve before reading/comparing proposed answer
- individually classify verified / needs_revision / rejected
- no invented color values or visual judgments
- full-fingerprint duplicate and answer-position checks
- only verified records may be promoted record-identically

## Cultural-history contract
Cultural-history questions are allowed because `色彩と文化` is an explicit current Grade 1 official section. Prefer concrete, source-auditable relations: material ↔ production method, period/culture ↔ documented color technology, documented artifact/conservation evidence ↔ factual conclusion. Avoid speculative symbolism, subjective appearance, approximate reconstructed palette, or broad claims that a fact is in the AFT textbook when only an external authority confirms it.

## Display contract
No monitor-facing Grade 1 item without an authoritative approved display reference. Printed-color second-stage tasks remain separate from text-only Grade 1 knowledge drills.
