# Grade 1 p.014 `日本の古代の色` source audit

Status: `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official boundary
The current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places `日本の色彩と文化 / 日本の古代の色` at p.014, after p.010 `近代ヨーロッパと色彩学` and before p.017 `古代から中近世の日本の色彩`. AFT public material therefore authorizes the topic and order, but does not publicly expose the p.014 body text or an exhaustive list of its facts.

## Resolved narrow facts
The following nonvisual historical/material facts are independently auditable from public institutional authority and may be used only within the stated boundary:

1. The Imperial Household Agency Shosoin Office states that most Shosoin treasures are eighth-century Nara-period objects, including items brought from the continent as well as objects made in Japan; the collection spans many crafts including textiles. This authorizes questions about the Shosoin/Nara-period/material-cultural relation, not a claim that every p.014 AFT example comes from Shosoin.
2. Kyoto National Museum states that ancient Japanese textile culture developed under influence from the Korean peninsula and China. For the Asuka and Nara periods it specifically identifies influences from Baekje, Goguryeo, Sui and Tang culture.
3. Kyoto National Museum states that textiles such as `錦`, `綾`, and `羅`, and dyed textiles made by techniques such as `臈纈`, `夾纈`, and `纐纈`, were brought to Japan or produced in Japan by following continental examples during the Asuka/Nara periods.
4. Kyoto National Museum identifies Horyuji textiles and Shosoin textiles as surviving bodies of material that transmit the appearance and techniques of ancient textiles to the present. This supports a narrow material-culture continuity question, not visual reconstruction of original colour appearance.
5. Kyoto National Museum collection records textually identify surviving Nara-period Shosoin textile fragments including `緑地唐花文錦`, `赤地唐花文錦`, and `淡青地葡萄唐草文綾`, and identify their material as silk. These catalogue titles authorize only the recorded names/period/material; do not infer measured colour coordinates, original appearance, dye chemistry, or AFT textbook membership from the images.

## Not authorized
Do not infer or author from this audit:
- an exhaustive list of p.014 AFT facts, colours, dye materials, court ranks, or garments;
- that every corroborated Shosoin/Horyuji textile fact appears verbatim in the AFT textbook;
- exact dye recipes, chemical composition, colour coordinates, RGB/HEX/display values, or reconstructed original hues unless separately source-audited;
- answers based on photographs or image appearance;
- universal symbolic meanings or social-rank rules without separate primary/public authority;
- facts belonging specifically to later p.017 or p.021 periods unless separately audited and aligned.

## Decision
Do not block the entire p.014 topic. AFT confirms the heading/order; Imperial Household Agency and Kyoto National Museum material resolve a narrow text-only Nara/Asuka textile-culture boundary covering Shosoin chronology, continental influence, named textile/dye-technique categories, and selected catalogue-record facts. Authoring may proceed only from those explicit relations. Additional p.014 facts require separate auditable source checks.
