# Power Color — contrast-tone authority audit: university / current education route

Checked: 2026-09-01

## Purpose
Continue the `pc3.relation.tone_difference` authority-elevation search without repeating the already exhausted generic J-STAGE/CiNii/KAKEN routes.

## New route checked
Targeted current university / vocational-school instructional material and exact PCCS `対照トーン` terminology.

Newly inspected sources include:
- Nippon Engineering College, 2024 `デザイン演習1` syllabus: https://www.neec.ac.jp/assets/contents/documents/portal/syllabus/hachioji/2024/portal_syllabus_hachioji_2024_24an014.pdf
- Nippon Engineering College, 2025 `デザイン演習B` syllabus: https://www.neec.ac.jp/assets/contents/documents/portal/syllabus/hachioji/2025/portal_syllabus_hachioji_2025_25la021.pdf
- NBS / NDG 2026 course disclosure: https://www.ndg-nbs.ac.jp/disclosure/2026/course_8.pdf
- 2026 peer-reviewed J-STAGE paper already known for the concrete `b` / `dk` contrast pair: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jsre/33/2/33_24-011/_html/-char/ja

## Finding
The current education materials independently confirm that PCCS tone-scheme instruction explicitly includes `同一トーン配色`, `類似トーン配色`, and `対照トーン配色`. This is useful scope corroboration, but the public syllabi do not enumerate the 12 chromatic tone pair-membership matrix or give a complete rule from which it can be reconstructed without inference.

The peer-reviewed 2026 paper remains pair-level evidence only (`b` / `dk`) and does not enumerate all memberships.

## Decision
- Do not generate `pc3.relation.tone_difference` from these materials.
- Do not promote the level-3 candidate grouping by inference.
- Mark the generic university/vocational syllabus route as exhausted unless a new source exposes an actual rule/table rather than course scope.
- The blocker remains authority elevation to a level-1/2 source that explicitly confirms the complete conventional grouping/matrix.
- Preferred remaining targets remain the current official Grade 3 text pp.84–89, equivalent JCRI/Japan Color Enterprise first-party material, current licensed PCCS Color Calc/related first-party output, or a genuinely new peer-reviewed/university source enumerating all 12 chromatic tone memberships.
