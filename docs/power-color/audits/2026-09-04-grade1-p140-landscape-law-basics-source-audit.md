# Grade 1 p.140 景観法の概要・特徴・景観計画について — source audit

Classification: `SOURCE_RESOLVED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places `景観法の概要`, `景観法の特徴`, and `景観計画について` at p.140 under `景観法とは`, followed by p.141 `景観計画に基づく届出と手続き`.

## Accepted authority
Current Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) material states that the Landscape Act provides mechanisms for action regulation and support measures to form good landscapes and enables systematic landscape policy suited to regional circumstances. MLIT's current landscape portal explains that a landscape plan may set restrictions on acts for good landscape formation and enables multiple regulatory/guidance mechanisms within the landscape-plan area. Its FAQ states that a landscape plan is a plan that a landscape administrative body may formulate under Article 8 of the Landscape Act for the purpose of forming a good landscape.

The current ministerial ordinance on landscape administrative bodies and landscape plans explicitly states that a landscape plan is represented by a plan map and a plan document.

These are direct government/legal authorities and resolve a narrow deterministic text-only p.140 boundary without relying on screen color, unpublished AFT wording, diagrams or inferred geometry.

## Authoring boundary
Text-only questions may test: the Landscape Act provides regulatory/support mechanisms for good landscape formation; landscape plans are formulated under Article 8 by landscape administrative bodies for good landscape formation; landscape plans can set restrictions on acts and enable regulatory/guidance mechanisms within their areas; a landscape plan is represented by a plan map and plan document. Do not import the detailed notification procedure reserved for p.141.
