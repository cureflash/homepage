# Grade 1 p.062 色名 — JISの光源色の色名 source audit

## Classification
`SOURCE_BLOCKED`

## Official scope/order
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places p.062 `JISの光源色の色名` after p.061 `ISCC-NBS色名法` and before p.062 `光源色の系統色名`. The next section after both p.062 topics is p.066 `測色 — 測色とは`.

## Current primary authority found
The Japanese Standards Association current catalog identifies `JIS Z 8110:1995 色の表示方法―光源色の色名 (Colour specification -- Names of light-source colour)` as valid, with confirmation date 2024-10-21. Its public abstract says the standard specifies names for colors of light emitted by light sources and perceived as luminous.

This resolves the current standard identity/status/scope only. The public JSA catalog does not expose the exact normative color-name content needed to author Grade 1 questions about the JIS light-source color names.

## Source routes checked
- AFT current Grade 1 official table of contents: resolves heading/order only.
- JSA current standard catalog: resolves current identity, validity, confirmation date, and abstract only.
- JISC/JIS general public search route: standard discovery/status route; no exact normative text established in this audit.
- Public secondary mirrors and explanatory sites were found that reproduce details of JIS Z 8110:1995, but these are not accepted as sole authority for exact current JIS content under the Grade 1 source contract.

## Rejected use
Do not author from secondary reproductions of the basic-color-name list, modifiers, abbreviations, chromaticity partitions, examples, or exact systematic-name rules. Do not infer current normative content merely because the 1995 standard remains valid.

## Root blocker
Exact current normative content of JIS Z 8110:1995 is not available from the accepted primary/public source route used here. The standard itself is commercially available from JSA, but the public catalog metadata is insufficient for deterministic Grade 1 authoring.

## Decision
No authoring. Revisit only if new explicit auditable primary authority exposes the relevant exact current normative content.
