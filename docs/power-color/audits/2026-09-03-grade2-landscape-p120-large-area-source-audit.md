# Grade 2 Landscape p.120 `大きな面積をもつ色彩` — source audit

Checked: 2026-09-03

## Result

SOURCE-BLOCKED for authoring.

The current Color Certification Association Grade 2 table of contents explicitly places `大きな面積をもつ色彩` at Landscape p.120 under `景観色彩における色彩の基礎知識`.

Publicly auditable material confirms only adjacent/general propositions: large-scale buildings can have large influence on surrounding streetscape and therefore require harmony consideration, and general color-design literature describes an area effect in which apparent color can change with viewing area. Those facts do not expose the official text's p.120 rule set or establish a sufficiently rich, closed, deterministic Grade 2 nonvisual boundary distinct from the already-promoted `pc2.landscape.long_lifecycle_color` statement that artificial landscape colors can occupy large visual areas.

## Boundary decision

Do not author a 12-question batch from any of the following alone:
- the heading `大きな面積をもつ色彩`;
- the already-promoted long-lifecycle large-area caution;
- generic large-building landscape-impact statements;
- generic area-effect explanations detached from an auditable Grade 2 landscape rule set.

Do not introduce RGB/HEX/display values, monitor swatches, inferred hue/tone/Munsell prescriptions, image appearance, unstated geometry, or numerical area thresholds.

Revisit only if explicit auditable authority exposes a distinct deterministic rule set sufficiently rich for independent Grade 2 questions.
