# Grade 1 p.072 色彩心理 — 心理学的尺度構成法の基本 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places p.072 `心理学的尺度構成法の基本` after p.069 `物理測色` and before p.073 `心理学的尺度構成法`. AFT public material establishes heading/order only.

## Accepted corroborating authority
- Tokyo Metropolitan University information-literacy material explicitly distinguishes nominal, ordinal, interval, and ratio scales and states the defining property of each.
- Osaka University MEPHAS scale guide independently states the same four-level structure and the meaningful relations for each scale.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. Nominal scale values distinguish categories; they do not imply order.
2. Ordinal scale values carry order/rank, but equal spacing is not guaranteed.
3. Interval scales preserve meaningful equal differences but do not have a meaningful absolute-zero ratio interpretation.
4. Ratio scales add a meaningful zero point and meaningful ratios to interval-scale properties.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not reconstruct unpublished AFT examples, questionnaire procedures, exact wording, numerical coding examples, statistical-treatment rules, scale-construction algorithms, diagrams, or later p.073/p.080 methods. Do not infer that any external example is necessarily the textbook example.

## Decision
Authoring is allowed only for the four deterministic nonvisual scale-level facts above. This does not assert exhaustive coverage of the unpublished p.072 textbook body.
