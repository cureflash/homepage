## Result

## Current official scope

The current Color Certification Association Grade 2 table of contents places `配色計画` on p.124 after p.123 `色を測ることによる周辺環境の把握` and before p.125 `色彩管理`. This confirms the current Grade 2 topic and order, but the public table of contents does not expose the detailed p.124 rule set.

The current Grade 2 official page/sample material confirms advanced color-scheme techniques and landscape application, but does not publicly expose a landscape-p.124 closed deterministic rule inventory.

## Auditable corroboration checked

The Japan Paint Manufacturers Association 2025 CCS practical seminar explicitly states that participants consolidate a color-design concept and then create a color-design/color-scheme proposal based on that concept. This supports `色彩設計コンセプト → 配色案`.

That relationship is already inside the accepted and promoted p.123 `pc2.landscape.residential_color_design_process` boundary. Recasting it as a separate p.124 12-question skill would duplicate an existing deterministic fact rather than add a distinct source-confirmed concept set.

Checked public-sector landscape material supplies surrounding-harmony, regional-context, numerical-criteria and landscape-review principles already represented by promoted p.117/p.122/p.123 skills. It does not expose the official text's distinct p.124 palette-selection rules, a closed scheme taxonomy, or fixed nonvisual decision rules.

## Prohibited inference

Do not infer fixed landscape palettes, hue/tone/Munsell/RGB/HEX values, image appearance, local regulatory limits as universal Grade 2 rules, unstated area ratios/geometry/illumination/tolerances, or a new skill made only of paraphrases of already-promoted facts.

## Decision

Do not author a distinct p.124 batch from current public evidence. Record `Landscape p.124 配色計画` as source-blocked until new explicit auditable authority exposes a sufficiently rich deterministic nonvisual boundary. Continue official text order to p.125 `色彩管理`.
