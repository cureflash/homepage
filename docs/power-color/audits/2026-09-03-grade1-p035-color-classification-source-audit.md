# Grade 1 p.035 色の分類 — source audit

Status: `SOURCE_BLOCKED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places `光と色 / 心理的な見えによる色の分類 / 色の分類` at p.035, immediately after p.032 `色の様相` and before p.036 `照明の性質を表すもの`.

Official TOC: https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf

## Source routes checked
1. Current AFT Grade 1 official scope/table-of-contents material. It confirms the heading and order but does not expose the p.035 body taxonomy.
2. CIE S 017:2020 e-ILV public definitions. CIE exposes multiple legitimate colour classifications and terms, including perceived-colour appearance modes, object/surface/aperture colour, luminous/non-luminous colour, related/unrelated colour, and chromatic/achromatic colour.
3. Japanese academic literature on colour-appearance modes, including reflected-surface versus light-source appearance and surrounding/depth effects.

## Why authoring is blocked
The public AFT material does not establish which exact classification axis, Japanese labels, hierarchy, or closed list p.035 requires. CIE provides several valid classification systems, but selecting one of them as the unpublished AFT p.035 taxonomy would reconstruct textbook content by inference. The p.032 accepted slice already covers appearance-mode facts and must not be duplicated under p.035 merely because related CIE terminology exists.

Therefore no p.035 question is authored from CIE/J-STAGE alone. Exact labels/counts/hierarchy remain blocked until AFT body authority or another source explicitly tying the classification to the current AFT p.035 scope is available.

## Rejected repeat routes
- Re-running generic searches for `色の分類` / appearance-mode terminology without new evidence tying a taxonomy to AFT p.035.
- Treating CIE's full colour-term inventory as if it were the AFT p.035 inventory.
- Reusing p.032 facts as nominally new p.035 questions.
