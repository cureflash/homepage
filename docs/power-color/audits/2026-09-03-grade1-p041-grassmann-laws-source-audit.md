## Auditable authority used
Current CIE authority:

- CIE S 017:2020 e-ILV `Grassmann's laws` defines three empirical laws describing colour-matching properties of additive mixtures of colour stimuli.
- Law 1: three independent variables are necessary and sufficient to specify a colour match.
- Law 2: for an additive mixture of colour stimuli, only the tristimulus values are relevant to the colour match, not the spectral compositions of the component stimuli.
- Law 3: when one or more mixture components are changed gradually, the resulting tristimulus values also change gradually.
- CIE explicitly notes that Grassmann's laws do not hold for all observing conditions.
- CIE e-ILV `colour equation` independently confirms the additive-mixture colour-matching representation using three reference colour stimuli.

## Accepted authoring boundary
Only the following deterministic nonvisual fact families are source-resolved enough to author:

1. Three independent variables are necessary and sufficient to specify a colour match.
2. For additive mixtures, colour matching depends on tristimulus values rather than the spectral compositions of the components.
3. Gradual change of one or more mixture components produces gradual change in the resulting tristimulus values.
4. The laws are empirical and are not guaranteed to hold under every observing condition.
