## Official scope

The current Color Certification Association Grade 2 table of contents places the next section after Interior p.112 at Landscape p.116:

- `景観の公共性`

The current official Grade 2 page also states that Grade 2 covers practical color coordination in specialist fields including landscape:
- https://www.aft.or.jp/pages/feature/level

## Narrow source-resolved nonvisual boundary

For p.116 `景観の公共性`, current Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism material explaining the Landscape Act explicitly states the statutory basic principle that good landscape is a common asset of the nation in the present and future, and that good landscape formation is to be advanced through integrated efforts by local public bodies, businesses and residents.

Government sources:
- https://www.mlit.go.jp/toshi/townscape/toshi_townscape_tk_000019.html
- https://www.hkd.mlit.go.jp/ky/jg/tosijyu/ud49g7000000lzxc.html

Accepted boundary for a future nonvisual micro-skill:
- `良好な景観` is treated as a present-and-future common national asset under the Landscape Act basic principles;
- good landscape formation is not an exclusively private matter and involves public/community cooperation;
- no color, hue, material, building-form, photograph, RGB/HEX, sampled-pixel, or unstated geometry inference is authorized by this audit.

## Not source-resolved here

- No deterministic Grade 2 question rule is fixed for the broad heading `景観とは` beyond the statutory/public-nature facts above.
- Do not turn current administrative counts or year-specific policy statistics into stable exam facts merely because they appear in recent MLIT material.
- Do not infer textbook-specific claims for `景観にかかわる近年の動向` from general policy history without a source tied closely enough to the Grade 2 content.

## Decision

p.116 is partially source-resolved only at the narrow public-nature boundary. The next safe authoring candidate after the p.112 runtime promotion is `pc2.landscape.public_nature`, limited to the explicit Landscape Act public-asset/cooperation facts above. It must still be created pending-first and independently re-solved before any runtime promotion.
