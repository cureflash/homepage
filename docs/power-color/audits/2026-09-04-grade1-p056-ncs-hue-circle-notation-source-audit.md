## Accepted authoring boundary
Only these deterministic nonvisual facts are source-resolved enough to author:
1. The NCS Colour Circle describes hue in relation to the four chromatic elementary colours.
2. In NCS notation, `R20B` denotes a red hue with 20% blue.
3. White, black and grey neutral colours have no hue and are notated with `-N` for neutral.
4. In the example `NCS S 1040-R20B`, `R20B` is the hue portion and `1040` is the nuance portion.
