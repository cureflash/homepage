# Grade 1 p.066 測色 — 測色とは source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official material places p.066 `測色 — 測色とは` after p.062 `光源色の系統色名` and before p.067 `視感測色―直接比較法`. AFT public material establishes heading/order only.

## Accepted corroborating authority
- CIE 015:2018 `Colorimetry, 4th Edition`, the current CIE technical report providing recommendations concerning colorimetry and explicitly listing standard colorimetric observers, standard illuminants, illuminating/viewing conditions, and calculation of tristimulus values, chromaticity coordinates, colour-space coordinates, and colour differences.
- ISO/CIE 11664-1:2019, which specifies standard colorimetric observers and their colour-matching functions for use in colorimetry.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. CIE colorimetry uses standard colorimetric observers and standard illuminants as standardized foundations.
2. Tristimulus values are among the quantities calculated in colorimetry.
3. Chromaticity coordinates are among the colorimetric quantities calculated from standardized colorimetric data.
4. Colour-space coordinates and colour differences are explicit parts of CIE colorimetric practice.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not reconstruct unpublished AFT definitions, wording, diagrams, worked examples, measurement-device procedures, spectral formulas, numerical tables, viewing geometry beyond explicitly public CIE text, RGB/HEX/display values, or monitor-facing judgments. Do not claim that the four corroborated fact families exhaust the AFT p.066 body text.

## Decision
Authoring is allowed only for the four deterministic nonvisual fact families above.
