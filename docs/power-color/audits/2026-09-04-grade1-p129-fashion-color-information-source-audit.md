# Grade 1 p.129 カラー情報の収集・調査 — source audit

Classification: `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places `カラー情報の収集・調査` at p.129 under `ファッションビジネスにおけるカラーコーディネーターの役割`. AFT public material does not expose the textbook's detailed collection procedure.

## Accepted corroboration
JAFCA is the Japanese color-trend system owner and publicly describes the information inputs used for its trend-color work. Its current `カラートレンド情報` page states that JAFCA uses (1) its own research into domestic consumer preferences and market trends and (2) information from Intercolor, the international colour-trend organisation. It also states that specialists familiar with each field form a committee that selects and communicates JAFCA fashion colors for Japanese industry.

Intercolor independently states that its member countries present seasonal concepts and views on changes in colour and lifestyle, exchange national proposals, discuss concepts/background, and agree seasonal colors/themes at meetings held twice a year.

These public facts resolve only a narrow information-source/research boundary. They do not resolve unpublished AFT-specific operational steps, forms, sampling rules, internal analysis method, or deliverables.

## Rejected routes
- Do not reconstruct an AFT workflow from generic fashion-business textbooks or study blogs.
- Do not infer survey design, sequence, weighting, or decision thresholds that JAFCA/AFT do not explicitly publish.
- Do not generate visual trend-color judgments.

## Authoring boundary
Text-only questions may test the explicit source categories: domestic consumer-preference research, market-trend research, Intercolor information, and the fact that these inputs support JAFCA's domestic trend-color selection. Detailed procedure remains out of scope.
