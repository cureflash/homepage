## Auditable corroborating authority

### Color Research & Application (Wiley) — Ostwald's Colour Harmony Manual

A 2023 peer-reviewed article comparing Munsell and Ostwald colour spaces contains a dedicated section `OSTWALD'S COLOUR HARMONY MANUAL`. It states that Ostwald used white content (W), black content (B), and full colour (C) as the three defining dimensions of his surface-colour system. It also describes the neutral black-to-white axis, the hue equator, the triangular sections connecting a pure colour to white and black, and the resulting double-cone structure.

The same section states that later versions used 24 hues and that harmony schemes could be defined by regular polygons whose vertices indicate harmonious couples, triples, quadruples, and sextuples. This is a direct, deterministic nonvisual relation between Ostwald's ordered colour system and his harmony schemes.

Source:
- https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/col.22818

## Authoring-safe fact families

A p.028 authoring checkpoint may use only these narrow corroborated relations:
1. Ostwald's surface-colour system uses white content (W), black content (B), and full colour (C) as defining dimensions.
2. With no black or white added in the pigment mixture, the system describes a pure/full colour.
3. The neutral axis runs from black to white and is perpendicular to the hue equator; pure-colour/white/black triangular sections form the double-cone structure.
4. In later 24-hue versions, harmony schemes were defined with regular polygons whose vertices identify harmonious pairs/couples, triples, quadruples, and sextuples.

These are safe as text-only historical/system relations. They do not establish the exact unpublished Japanese terminology, diagrams, notation, category ordering, or examples used by AFT p.028.
