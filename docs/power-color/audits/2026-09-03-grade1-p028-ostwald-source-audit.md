## Auditable corroborating authority

### Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum — Ostwald colour system and harmony

Cooper Hewitt's discussion of Wilhelm Ostwald's `Farbmesstafel` and colour theory states that:
- Ostwald began sustained work on colour theory and organisation after retirement in 1906.
- In 1916 he wrote `The Colour Primer`.
- The system was devoted to relationships between colours and proposed that harmony is created by colour order.
- One scale is achromatic, running from white through greys to black.
- Another scale is based on eight principal hues.
- Ostwald represented the theory in three dimensions as a double cone.
- He used numbering/lettering to designate amounts of achromatic components in colour classifications.

Source:
- https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2018/05/09/the-chemistry-of-color/

### Science History Institute — original `Die Farbenfibel` object record

Science History Institute's digitized public-domain `Die Farbenfibel` record identifies Wilhelm Ostwald as creator and describes the guide as containing sections on achromatic and chromatic colours, bright/dark shades, murky colours, and colour harmony. The surviving digitized edition is an original-work record and supports the existence and scope of Ostwald's colour guide without relying on exam-prep summaries.

Source:
- https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/die-farbenfibel-ostwald-wilhelm-1853-1932/WgHtR1o_uStCNA
