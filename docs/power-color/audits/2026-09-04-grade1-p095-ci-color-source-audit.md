# Power Color Grade 1 — p.095 企業アイデンティティ戦略における色彩 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Result
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents fixes p.095 as `コンシューマプロダクツのビジネスプロセスと色彩 — 企業アイデンティティ戦略における色彩`, immediately before p.096 `商品企画における色彩`. Public AFT material does not expose the unpublished p.095 textbook body or an AFT-specific CI process/sequence.

A narrow deterministic nonvisual boundary is nevertheless auditable without reconstructing that unpublished process. J-Net21 (独立行政法人中小企業基盤整備機構) defines corporate identity (CI) as an enterprise strategy that re-identifies/reconstructs a company's characteristics and uniqueness and expresses them through unified image/design/message. It explicitly distinguishes MI, BI, and VI, and lists `コーポレートカラー` among VI examples together with symbol marks, logos, store design, packages, and printed matter. J-Net21 also explicitly warns that CI is not merely replacing a mark/logo; such visual renewal is one means inside a broader strategy.

As a current system-owner example, Hitachi announced in March 2025 that its renewed visual identity consists of six design elements, including a new color palette alongside a simplified logo, proprietary typeface, image guidelines, graphic texture, and template layout. This corroborates the limited proposition that color can be managed as one explicit element of a unified visual identity.

## Accepted fact families
1. CI is a broader enterprise strategy, not merely logo replacement.
2. VI is the visual-expression side of CI, and corporate color is an explicit VI element.
3. MI concerns philosophy/mental direction, BI concerns putting philosophy/strategy into behavior, while VI handles visual expression; color belongs to the VI side in the accepted public definition.
4. A current corporate VI can explicitly manage a color palette as one design element within a unified brand-design system.

## Excluded / blocked
- Unpublished AFT p.095 wording, diagrams, CI process steps, sequence, required deliverables, examples, or exact color-selection rules.
- Any claim that the accepted external fact families exhaust the AFT p.095 content.
- RGB/HEX, monitor swatches, visual appearance judgments, or inferred brand-color psychology.

Author only four text-only records from the accepted fact families above. Keep the item classified `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`; do not infer an AFT-specific CI workflow.
