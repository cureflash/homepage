# Grade 2 Landscape p.120 `人工要素と自然要素の色彩が共存` source audit

Checked: 2026-09-03

## Official scope/order

Current Color Certification Association Grade 2 material remains the primary scope authority.

- Grade 2 overview: https://www.aft.or.jp/pages/feature/level
- Current Grade 2 text table of contents: https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-2st-grade_mokuji.pdf

The official table of contents explicitly places `人工要素と自然要素の色彩が共存` at p.120 under `景観色彩における色彩の基礎知識`, followed by `ライフサイクルの長い色彩`, `大きな面積をもつ色彩`, and `太陽光に影響される色彩`.

## Public corroboration checked

A current peer-reviewed Japanese Design Science Association article classifies visual targets in an urban park into categories including artificial elements and natural elements, confirming that these are legitimate landscape-analysis categories:

- https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jssdj/71/4/71_4_1/_article/-char/ja

A professional architectural-color reference explicitly describes landscape as including artificial elements such as buildings, roads and bridges and natural elements such as trees, soil, sea and rivers, with color present in those elements:

- https://www.kenchikucolor.com/

These corroborate the broad concept expressed by the official p.120 heading, but they do not expose the official text's detailed rule set, intended example inventory, or examination-specific distinctions.

## Decision

Source-block authoring for this heading for now.

The publicly auditable evidence is sufficient only for the broad proposition that landscape contains both artificial and natural elements and that color is present in them. That proposition alone does not support a sufficiently rich, closed, deterministic Grade 2 micro-skill without either:

- inventing an external example taxonomy;
- treating one professional site's examples as if they were the official-text inventory;
- inferring color attributes or appearance from images/materials; or
- producing low-value paraphrase duplicates of the heading.

None of those is allowed under the current Power Color source gate.

## Allowed facts

The following may remain as source-confirmed structural context, not as a 12-question authoring contract:

- the official Grade 2 text has this p.120 topic under landscape-color fundamentals;
- artificial and natural elements are valid landscape-analysis categories;
- the topic concerns coexistence of color-bearing artificial and natural landscape elements.

## Not authorized

Do not infer or author:

- a closed official list of artificial/natural examples;
- any fixed color associated with any element;
- hue, tone, Munsell, RGB/HEX or monitor-facing values;
- material appearance, photograph appearance or unstated geometry;
- a rule that every external example belongs to the official Grade 2 text.

## Exact next source frontier

Continue official-text order to p.120 `ライフサイクルの長い色彩`. Source-acquire an explicit deterministic nonvisual rule boundary before authoring. Do not revisit this heading unless new explicit auditable authority appears.
