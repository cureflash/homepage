# Power Color Grade 1 — p.030 ムーンとスペンサーの色彩調和論 source audit

Date: 2026-09-03
Status: `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope authority

The current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places `ムーンとスペンサーの色彩調和論` at p.030, after p.029 Itten and before p.032 `色の様相`.

AFT TOC:
- https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf

The public AFT material does not expose the complete p.030 textbook body, so exact AFT wording, equations, thresholds, diagrams, and exercise membership are not treated as resolved.

## Auditable corroborating authority

### Japan Color Science Association / J-STAGE
A 2019 paper published in the Japan Color Science Association proceedings states that Moon & Spencer defined harmony and ambiguity regions in Munsell color space and defined an aesthetic measure (`美度`) from order and complexity. This is sufficient for a narrow nonvisual conceptual boundary, but not for reconstructing AFT-specific equations or threshold values.

Source:
- https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jcsaj/43/3%2B/43_148/_article/-char/ja

### National Diet Library / Optica bibliographic authority
NDL records Moon and Domina Eberle Spencer's `Area in Color Harmony` as a 1944 Journal of the Optical Society of America article, volume 34 issue 2, beginning at p.93, published by Optica Publishing Group.

Source:
- https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000136-I1363951794451990784

### CiNii / Optica bibliographic authority
CiNii records the companion 1944 JOSA papers `Geometric Formulation of Classical Color Harmony` and `Aesthetic Measure Applied to Color Harmony`. Together with the NDL record, these establish the three 1944 Moon-Spencer paper titles without relying on exam-prep summaries.

Sources:
- https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1571980075686695680
- https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1363670320741783808

### Peer-reviewed later evaluation of the spatial-balance rule
A peer-reviewed Journal of General Psychology paper summarizes Moon and Spencer's 1944 spatial-balance model as treating both chroma and value as contributors to balance, with contrast against the background/adaptation point as the dominant factor. This is accepted only as later scholarly corroboration of the model's broad nonvisual content.

Source:
- https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00221309.1987.9711073

## Authoring-safe fact families

A p.030 authoring checkpoint may use only these narrow nonvisual relations:
1. Moon & Spencer's theory uses Munsell color space and defines harmony/ambiguity regions.
2. Their `美度`/aesthetic-measure concept is built from order and complexity; do not reconstruct an unpublished AFT formula or threshold.
3. Their 1944 JOSA work includes `Geometric Formulation of Classical Color Harmony`, `Area in Color Harmony`, and `Aesthetic Measure Applied to Color Harmony`.
4. Later peer-reviewed literature summarizes their spatial-balance model as involving chroma and value, with contrast against the background/adaptation point as the dominant factor.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not author or infer:
- the complete unpublished AFT p.030 body;
- exact AFT Japanese terminology, equations, coefficients, numerical thresholds, harmony/ambiguity interval boundaries, or diagrams not exposed by current public AFT authority;
- RGB/HEX/display values, image appearance, or reconstructed geometry;
- exact area-ratio calculations from unpublished AFT examples;
- any claim that later empirical support or criticism is itself AFT textbook content;
- p.032 `色の様相` content.
