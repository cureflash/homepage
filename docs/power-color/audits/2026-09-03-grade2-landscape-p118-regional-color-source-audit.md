# Grade 2 Landscape p.118 `地域には地域の色がある` source audit

Checked: 2026-09-03

## Official Grade 2 alignment

The current Color Certification Association Grade 2 official text table of contents places p.118 `地域の気候風土がつくりだす景観色彩` → `地域には地域の色がある` immediately after p.117 `景観色彩における色彩設計の役割`. The same page next lists `国内外の景観色彩の事例`.

Primary scope/order authority:
- `aft_grade2_current_toc_2026` — current Color Certification Association Grade 2 official text table of contents, rechecked 2026-09-03.

The public AFT material fixes the Grade 2 scope/order but does not publicly expose enough textbook prose to derive detailed nonvisual questions from this heading alone. Therefore the rule boundary below is limited to propositions independently and explicitly stated by auditable institutional/professional sources.

## Auditable rule sources

Accepted institutional/professional sources:
- `jfa_regional_color_journal_2016` — Japan Fashion Association, `生活文化創造都市ジャーナル_vol.2 まちづくりと景観色彩`, current public page rechecked 2026-09-03.
- `jcca_consultant269_regional_color` — Japan Civil Engineering Consultants Association, journal `Consultant 269`, p.28 `まちなみと煉瓦 色彩・景観デザインの視点から見た煉瓦の可能性`, current public page rechecked 2026-09-03.

The Japan Fashion Association article explicitly states that traditional preserved streetscapes are basically built using natural materials produced in their regions and are organized by colors nurtured by the regional climate and natural environment. It presents this as an example of the proposition that regions have their own colors and describes traditional regionally produced material/color relationships as a useful reference for future urban development.

The JCCA article independently explains an environmental-color method in which planners learn the colors of things that have long existed or accumulated in a region and respect them in order to nurture a region-specific landscape. It identifies this method with Jean-Philippe Lenclos's color-geography approach and the proposition `地域には地域の色がある`.

These sources establish a deterministic nonvisual relationship boundary without requiring any judgment from photographs or monitor colors.

## Accepted micro-skill boundary

Authorize `pc2.landscape.regional_color_context` only for text questions about the following explicit propositions:
1. `地域には地域の色がある` concerns relationships between a region's long-established/accumulated colors and its landscape, not a universal region-to-one-color lookup table;
2. in traditional preserved streetscapes described by the accepted source, regionally produced natural materials contribute to the local color composition;
3. regional climate and natural environment are connected with the colors seen in those traditional streetscapes;
4. an environmental-color planning method is to learn colors that have long existed or accumulated in a region and respect them when nurturing a region-specific landscape;
5. the concept therefore supports starting from regional context/history/material accumulation rather than selecting unrelated colors solely by arbitrary preference.

Questions may test only these relationships or distinguish them from clearly unsupported alternatives. They must not require a learner to identify the actual color of any named locality.

## Exclusions

This audit does not authorize:
- any fixed `地域名 → 色名` mapping;
- any RGB/HEX/display value or monitor-facing swatch question;
- any specific hue, tone, value, chroma or Munsell notation for a region or material;
- any answer derived from a photograph, screenshot, sampled pixel or image appearance;
- any claim that all buildings in a region use the same local material or color;
- any universal deterministic direction such as `north = dark` or `west = light`;
- any material-color appearance inference not explicitly stated in the accepted text;
- any recommendation that a particular new building must copy a specific traditional color.

The source contract is conceptual and relational only. If later questions need actual regional colors, material colors, colorimetry or monitor display, a separate authoritative source/display gate is required.
