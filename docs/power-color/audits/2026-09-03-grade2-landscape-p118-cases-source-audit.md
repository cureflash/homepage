## Public-source result

Current public first-party material confirms that landscape color is in Grade 2 and fixes the official-text order, but it does not expose a closed textual list of the p.118 domestic/overseas examples together with deterministic nonvisual answer rules.

Public-sector landscape guidance can document particular places, regulations and design standards, but substituting arbitrary external case studies for the official-text examples would change the tested content. Many candidate case questions would also depend on photographs, sampled appearance, unstated hue/tone/Munsell values, or inferred visual geometry.

## Gate decision

`国内外の景観色彩の事例` is source-blocked for question authoring under the current gate.

Do not author questions that require:
- identifying an official-text case from an external case-study list;
- inferring a place from image appearance;
- inferring hue, tone, Munsell, RGB/HEX or material color from photographs;
- assuming that a public-sector example is one of the official textbook's examples without explicit evidence.

No monitor-facing or image-derived question is authorized by this audit.
