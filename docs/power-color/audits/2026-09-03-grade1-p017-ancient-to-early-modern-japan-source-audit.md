# Grade 1 p.017 `古代から中近世の日本の色彩` source audit

Status: `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official boundary
The current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places `古代から中近世の日本の色彩` at p.017, after p.014 `日本の古代の色` and before p.021 `近代の日本の色彩`. AFT public material authorizes the topic and order, but does not publicly expose the p.017 body text or an exhaustive list of its examples/facts.

## Resolved narrow facts
The following text-only historical/cultural facts are auditable from public university/library and Agency for Cultural Affairs cultural-property authority and may be used only within the stated boundary:

1. Okayama Prefectural University Library explains `襲色目` as a Heian-period court/aristocratic colour-coordination practice. It distinguishes the colour pairing of the face/back of one lined garment (`合わせの色目`) from the combined colour effect of layered garments such as women’s court dress (`襲袿色目`), and states that colour coordination was considered in relation to seasons and events.
2. Ochanomizu University’s institutional repository and CiNii/IRDB record a university research report specifically titled `平安貴族社会における「襲の色目」`, corroborating that `襲の色目` is treated as a historical topic of Heian aristocratic society. The metadata alone does not authorize exact individual colour-pair names or reconstructed values.
3. Agency for Cultural Affairs `文化遺産オンライン` records `熨斗雪輪模様小袖` as an early-Edo, 17th-century kosode using a dark-brown figured-silk ground with kanoko tie-dye, stitched-resist dyeing and embroidery.
4. Agency for Cultural Affairs `文化遺産オンライン` records `蛇籠桜樹模様小袖` as a mid-Edo, 18th-century kosode using a blue-grey (`納戸`) crepe ground with yuzen dyeing, embroidery and surihitta.
5. Agency for Cultural Affairs `文化遺産オンライン` records `蒲公英蝶模様小袖` as a late-Edo, 19th-century kosode on yellow figured silk using yuzen and stencil dyeing; the catalogue explanation explicitly notes incorporation of the hem-pattern fashion that became popular from the mid-Edo period onward.

These facts authorize a narrow progression from Heian court colour coordination to documented Edo kosode colour/technique records. They do not establish that these exact examples exhaust or reproduce the unpublished AFT p.017 text.

## Not authorized
Do not infer or author from this audit:
- exhaustive AFT p.017 membership, chronology, colour-name list, court-rank rules or symbolic meanings;
- exact `襲の色目` pair tables, individual pair names, seasonal assignments, Munsell/PCCS/RGB/HEX/display values, or reconstructed historical appearance unless separately source-audited;
- colour values or technique details from photographs or image appearance;
- a universal claim that one kosode example represents all Edo-period dress;
- unsupported transitions from Heian court dress to later warrior/commoner dress;
- facts that belong specifically to p.021 modern Japan unless separately audited.

## Decision
Do not block the entire p.017 topic. AFT confirms the heading/order. University/library authority resolves a narrow Heian `襲の色目` structural and seasonal/event context, while Agency for Cultural Affairs catalogue records resolve specific text-only Edo kosode period/material/technique relations. Authoring may proceed only from these explicit relations; additional p.017 facts require separate auditable source checks.
