## Auditable authority used
- Liberini et al., *Color Research & Application* (2023), `Munsell and Ostwald colour spaces: A comparison in the field of hair colouring`: describes Ostwald surface colors with white content W, black content B, and full color C; states W+B+C=1; explains triangular charts with white, black and full color; and describes notation combining hue identification with white/black-content letters. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/col.22818
- Illuminating Engineering Society glossary, `Ostwald color system`: defines the system as describing colors in terms of color content, white content and black content, commonly shown with triangular charts whose apexes are full color, white and black. https://ies.org/definitions/ostwald-color-system/
- Japan Color Science Association 2024 conference material hosted by Ritsumeikan University: states the Ostwald-system idea that colors can be described by the composition ratio of white, black, and pure color. https://www.psy.ritsumei.ac.jp/akitaoka/shikisai2024.html

## Accepted authoring boundary
Only these deterministic nonvisual facts are source-resolved enough to author:
1. Ostwald describes surface colors using white content, black content, and full/pure color content.
2. The normalized three contents satisfy W + B + C = 1 (equivalently 100%).
3. A triangular chart represents full color, white, and black at its three apexes.
4. In the 24-step version, notation communicates hue together with white- and black-content information; exact AFT symbols/examples remain blocked.
