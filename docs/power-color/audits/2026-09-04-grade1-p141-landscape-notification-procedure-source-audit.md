# Grade 1 p.141 景観計画に基づく届出と手続き — source audit

Classification: `SOURCE_RESOLVED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places p.141 `景観計画に基づく届出と手続き` immediately after the p.140 landscape-law basics and before p.142 `色彩基準の役割`.

## Accepted authority
Current MLIT material on electronic handling of Landscape Act procedures explicitly identifies three statutory procedure categories: `行為の届出（法16条1項）`, `変更届（法16条2項）`, and `行為の通知（法16条5項）`. This resolves the legal procedure-name/article mapping without reconstructing unpublished AFT textbook wording.

Current municipal government guidance implementing the same Landscape Act framework additionally confirms the practical distinction: ordinary covered acts require a Landscape Act notification before commencement, a later plan change requires a change notification, and acts by national or local public bodies use a prior notification route rather than the ordinary private-party filing route. These public-government sources are used only to corroborate the statutory mapping already explicit in MLIT material.

## Authoring boundary
Text-only questions may test only the deterministic mapping among: Article 16(1) = action notification, Article 16(2) = change notification, Article 16(5) = action notification by national/local public bodies, and the fact that these are distinct Landscape Act notification/notice procedures. Do not author municipality-specific thresholds, local preliminary-consultation rules, local forms, color standards, visual judgments, RGB/HEX, or unpublished AFT process detail.

## Rejected routes
- Do not generalize local thresholds, document counts, or pre-consultation procedures into nationwide AFT rules.
- Do not infer color-standard content from p.141; p.142 onward is audited separately.
- Do not use diagram appearance or unstated geometry as authority.
