## Resolved nonvisual fact boundary
MLIT explicitly states that the Outdoor Advertisement Act provides the legal basis while actual outdoor-advertising regulation is carried out independently by local governments through ordinances and rules. Tokyo's current official guide separates general permit criteria from standards for specified areas and also records landscape-plan-based regulation. Kyoto states that the whole city is designated as prohibited areas, 21 types of regulation zones, or special outdoor-advertising regulation districts, with permission procedures required before display in regulated areas/districts.

This resolves a narrow structural p.145 slice: outdoor-advertising color/design standards are not one nationwide numerical color table; applicable criteria depend on the local regulatory framework and, where provided, the relevant area/district. The authoring batch tests only that governance/area-specific structure and does not reconstruct AFT's unpublished examples or exact color thresholds.

## Exclusions
Do not infer exact AFT color limits, Tokyo/Kyoto numerical hue/value/chroma thresholds, visual acceptability, RGB/HEX, monitor-facing judgments, sign geometry, or a nationwide universal color threshold. Municipality-specific exact standards require their own current explicit authority and are not imported into this batch.
