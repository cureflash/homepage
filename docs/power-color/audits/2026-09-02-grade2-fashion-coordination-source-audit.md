## Rule audit

The reviewed public first-party material confirms the topic and its place in the current Grade 2 text, but does not expose the detailed p.095 rule set.

Current public Grade 2 preparation material independently confirms that `ファッションの配色` is taught as a dedicated lesson, but the accessible descriptions do not expose a sufficiently explicit deterministic mapping that can be converted into Power Color text-choice records without importing unstated textbook content.

General fashion-color sources were also reviewed only as search leads. They contain advice such as base/main/accent color proportions or example combinations, but these are not adequate authority for the specific current Color Certification Grade 2 p.095 answer rules. They therefore must not be converted into exam facts.

## Decision

`ファッションの配色` p.095 is source-blocked for authoring at this checkpoint.

Do not infer a Grade 2 rule from outfit photographs, garment area ratios, example hues, current trends, personal-color systems, swatches, RGB/HEX, sampled pixels, or unstated composition geometry.

The previously promoted generic scheme skills remain valid only within their separately sourced rule boundaries; do not relabel them as p.095 fashion-specific facts without an explicit source.

## Exact continuation

Skip p.095 authoring unless a future explicit auditable rule source is acquired. Continue official-text-order source acquisition at `ファッションにおける配色の事例` p.096, and if no deterministic nonvisual rule is exposed there, continue to `インテリア概念の成立` p.104 without generating from images or examples.
