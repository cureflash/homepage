## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places `色彩心理 — 色の心理的効果 — 記憶色と色記憶` at p.082, after p.080 `心理物理学的測定法` and before p.083 `色の見えの変化`. Public AFT material establishes topic membership/order only; the textbook body is not public and is not reconstructed here.

## Auditable corroboration used
- AFT current Grade 1 textbook table of contents: official heading/order only.
- Hansen, Olkkonen, Walter & Gegenfurtner, *Nature Neuroscience* 9 (2006), `Memory modulates color appearance`: observers adjusted familiar fruit images to appear achromatic; settings were shifted opposite the fruit's typical colour, showing modulation of colour appearance by high-level visual memory.
- Bartleson, *Journal of the Optical Society of America* 50 (1960), `Memory Colors of Familiar Objects`: mean memory colours for ten familiar natural objects differed significantly from corresponding natural colours and tended to be more characteristic of the object's dominant chromatic attribute.
- Pérez-Carpinell et al., *Color Research & Application* 23 (1998), `Color memory matching: Time effect and other factors`: memory/successive colour matching generally produced larger mean colour differences than simultaneous matching, and delay time (15 s, 15 min, 24 h) significantly affected the remembered mean colour.

## Accepted fact families
1. Familiar-object colour appearance can be modulated by stored knowledge of the object's typical colour.
2. In the Hansen fruit experiment, an object generally appeared achromatic only after adjustment in the direction opposite its typical colour.
3. Bartleson's familiar-object memory colours were not exact replicas of measured natural-object colours and tended toward a more characteristic dominant chromatic attribute.
4. In Pérez-Carpinell's colour-memory matching experiment, memory/successive matching generally showed larger mean colour differences than simultaneous matching, and remembered mean colour depended significantly on delay time.
