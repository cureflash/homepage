# Grade 1 p.143 `色彩基準の運用` source audit

Classification: `SOURCE_RESOLVED`

## Official scope
Current AFT Grade 1 textbook table of contents places `景観計画における色彩基準 — 色彩基準の運用` at p.143. The public TOC establishes membership/order only; unpublished AFT body wording is not reconstructed.

## Resolved nonvisual fact boundary
Tokyo's official guideline explicitly states that Munsell hue/value/chroma gives an objective color indication, while actual appearance can vary with surface condition, transmission and reflection. For tile/stone and other materials with large color variation, advance consultation should use actual samples where possible so material condition can be checked together with color. For particularly large buildings, large mock-ups are an available method for sharing the completion image during consultation. Materials with rich texture/variation may require comprehensive judgment on application of the color standard rather than a single numerical reading alone.

These facts directly resolve a narrow operational p.143 slice without inferring unpublished AFT procedure.

## Exclusions
Do not infer municipality-specific forms, submission timing, exact numerical color limits, area ratios, exceptions, RGB/HEX, monitor appearance, or unpublished AFT sequence/terminology. p.143 `色彩基準における面積比の考え方` is audited separately.
