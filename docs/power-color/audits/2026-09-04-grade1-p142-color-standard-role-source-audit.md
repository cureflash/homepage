# Grade 1 p.142 景観計画における色彩基準 — 色彩基準の役割 — source audit

Classification: `SOURCE_RESOLVED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places p.142 `色彩基準の役割` under `景観計画における色彩基準`, immediately after p.141 notification/procedure and before p.143 `色彩基準の運用`.

## Accepted authority
Current MLIT material states that a landscape plan may prescribe restrictions on acts for good landscape formation and thereby enable regulatory/guidance mechanisms within the landscape-plan area. MLIT also explains that landscape-formation standards should, as far as possible, be objective standards, because they are used in setting areas/restrictions and in judging dispositions under Landscape Act operation.

Current Tokyo Metropolitan Government guidance gives the color-specific operational role without requiring screen judgment: landscape-formation standards are part of the action restrictions applied through the notification system, and the Tokyo landscape color guideline separates numerical `基準編` from `活用編`, requiring not only conformity to numerical standards but also color planning that reflects the purpose of those standards and harmony with the surrounding streetscape.

Current Osaka Prefecture guidance likewise states that the landscape plan sets restrictions for good landscape formation and that those restrictions include the form and color of buildings, with notification used for regulatory guidance.

Together these government sources resolve a narrow deterministic p.142 boundary: color standards function as part of landscape-plan restrictions/guidance for evaluating and guiding notified acts toward good landscape formation; numerical conformity alone does not substitute for the planning purpose/context where the administering authority explicitly says so.

## Authoring boundary
Text-only questions may test only: landscape-plan standards are part of action restrictions/guidance for good landscape formation; color can be an explicit subject of those restrictions; standards are used as judgment/guidance criteria; and current Tokyo guidance distinguishes numerical-standard conformity from the broader need to consider the standard's purpose and surrounding harmony. Do not author exact hue/value/chroma limits, municipality-specific numeric thresholds, area ratios, exceptions, visual judgments, RGB/HEX, or unpublished AFT wording.

## Rejected routes
- Do not infer p.143 operational procedures, p.143 area-ratio rules, p.144 presentation methods, or p.145 exceptions from p.142.
- Do not convert local numerical standards into nationwide/AFT values.
- Do not use images, rendered colors, or unstated geometry as authority.
