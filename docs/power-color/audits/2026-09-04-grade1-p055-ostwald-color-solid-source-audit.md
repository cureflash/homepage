## Official scope anchor
The current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places p.055 `色立体` after p.054 `等色相面` and before p.056 NCS `へリングの6主要色`. Public AFT material establishes this heading/order but does not expose the p.055 body text, exact figure, geometry, notation layout, or worked examples.

## Auditable authority used
- AFT current official Grade 1 textbook table of contents: establishes the exact heading/order only. https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf
- Walter C. Granville, *Color Research & Application* 19(2), 77–98 (1994), `The Color Harmony Manual, a Color Atlas Based on the Ostwald Color System`: the peer-reviewed abstract explicitly states that the Ostwald hue triangles and a color solid made with them portray the sensory world of color; important clear sequences lie on the outside surface of the solid; and color scales of equal blackness and whiteness are defined. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1520-6378.1994.tb00068.x

## Accepted authoring boundary
Only these deterministic nonvisual facts are source-resolved enough to author:
1. The Ostwald color solid is constructed from / represented by the system's hue triangles rather than being an unrelated separate classification.
2. Important clear-color sequences lie on the outside surface of the color solid.
3. The system defines scales of equal blackness and scales of equal whiteness in relation to the color solid.

These facts establish only broad structural relations. They do not authorize reconstructing the current AFT p.055 figure.
