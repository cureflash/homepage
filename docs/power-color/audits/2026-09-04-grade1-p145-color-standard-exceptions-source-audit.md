# Grade 1 p.145 `色彩基準の適用除外` source audit

Classification: `SOURCE_RESOLVED`

## Official scope
Current AFT Grade 1 textbook table of contents places `景観計画における色彩基準 — 色彩基準の適用除外` at p.145, immediately after p.144 `色彩基準の示し方`. The public TOC establishes membership/order only; unpublished AFT body wording is not reconstructed.

## Accepted authority
- AFT current Grade 1 TOC: https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf
- 東京都都市整備局 `東京都景観色彩ガイドライン`: https://www.toshiseibi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/documents/d/toshiseibi/pdf_keikaku_keikan_shingikai-045_tokyo_color_guideline_01_02

## Resolved nonvisual fact boundary
Tokyo's official guideline explicitly lists cases in which its color standard need not be followed. After hearing the opinion of the landscape council or similar body, these include an area where region-specific color standards are already established and good landscape formation is achieved; use of region-specific natural materials such as natural stone and richly textured tile, brick or wood; a bridge or similar structure familiar to residents and functioning as a regional landmark; and another color plan that contributes to good landscape formation and implementation of the plan.

The guideline also states that structures whose color is prescribed by other laws, and amusement facilities without a part recognizable as a wall surface, may be outside the color standard. Uncolored glass not perceived as high-chroma may also be outside the standard because it reflects surrounding landscape and sky and its color is not constant, while the standard's underlying concept must still be respected.

These explicit government facts directly resolve a narrow p.145 exception slice.

## Exclusions
All examples in authoring are explicitly Tokyo-guideline examples; do not universalize them as nationwide rules. Do not infer visual appearance, RGB/HEX, exact material colors, unpublished AFT exception wording, or any p.145 outdoor-advertising rule from this audit.
