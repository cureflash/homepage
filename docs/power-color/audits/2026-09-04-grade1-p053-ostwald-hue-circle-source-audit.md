## Auditable authority used
- AFT current Grade 1 level/textbook materials: official scope/order only.
- Musashino Art University Art & Design Glossary, `Hue Circle`: states that the Ostwald system has eight principal hues, each divided into three, giving 24 hues; colors opposite on the Ostwald hue circle are complementary and their combination produces an achromatic color. https://art-design-glossary.musabi.ac.jp/hue-circle/
- Liberini et al., *Color Research & Application* (2023), `Munsell and Ostwald colour spaces: A comparison in the field of hair colouring`: corroborates that later Ostwald versions comprise 24 hues and that yellow is opposite ultramarine blue while red is opposite sea green. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/col.22818
- Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities (BBAW), Wilhelm-Ostwald-Symposium archive, Ostwald atlas sample: archival material visibly documents the hue-circle/atlas context and a numbered hue sample. https://ostwald.bbaw.de/fileadmin/Webdateien/Dateien/BerlinBrand_Ak_04.pdf

## Accepted authoring boundary
Only these deterministic nonvisual facts are resolved enough to author:
1. The Ostwald hue circle used here consists of 24 hues.
2. Eight principal hues are each subdivided into three groups/steps to form the 24-hue circle.
3. Hues opposite one another on the Ostwald hue circle are complementary; their combination is described as producing an achromatic color.
4. In the corroborated opponent arrangement, yellow is opposite ultramarine blue and red is opposite sea green.
