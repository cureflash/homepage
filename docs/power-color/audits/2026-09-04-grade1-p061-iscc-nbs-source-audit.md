# Grade 1 p.061 色名 — ISCC-NBS色名法 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official material places p.061 `色名 — ISCC-NBS色名法` after p.060 `色を表す言葉の分類` and before p.062 JIS light-source color-name topics. AFT public material establishes heading/order only.

## Accepted corroborating authority
- NIST publication record for Kenneth L. Kelly and Deane B. Judd, `Color: Universal Language and Dictionary of Names`, NBS Special Publication 440 (1976), which supersedes/combines the earlier ISCC-NBS method/dictionary.
- Journal of Research of the National Bureau of Standards, Kenneth L. Kelly, `Central Notations for the Revised ISCC-NBS Color-Name Blocks`, Vol. 61 No. 5 (1958), NIST-hosted. This explicitly states the revised system has 267 color-name blocks, revised in 1955, and describes their Munsell hue/value/chroma boundaries and rapid-use purpose of central colors.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. The revised ISCC-NBS system contains 267 color-name blocks.
2. The color-name blocks were revised in 1955 and published in NBS Circular 553.
3. An elementary block is bounded in Munsell terms by hue, value, and chroma limits.
4. A chart of the central colors of the 267 blocks was described as a quicker way to determine ISCC-NBS designations for nonborderline colors than first determining a Munsell renotation and then consulting boundary charts.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not reconstruct unpublished AFT examples, exact modifier inventory/order, all 267 names, numerical boundary tables, centroids, swatches, RGB/HEX, display appearance, or monitor-facing judgments. Do not treat the historical NBS documents as proof of AFT textbook wording.

## Decision
Authoring is allowed only for the four deterministic nonvisual fact families above.
