# Grade 1 p.080 色彩心理 — 心理物理学的測定法 source audit

## Classification

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places p.080 `心理物理学的測定法` after p.073 `心理学的尺度構成法` and before p.082 `記憶色と色記憶`. AFT public material establishes the heading/order but does not expose the unpublished textbook procedures or exact method inventory.

## Accepted corroborating authority
Tohoku Gakuin University educational psychology material explicitly identifies three classical psychophysical measurement methods: `恒常（刺激）法`, `極限法`, and `調整法`. Its method descriptions further state that in the adjustment method the participant adjusts the comparison stimulus, while the constant-stimuli and limits methods differ in stimulus presentation/selection procedures.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. Three classical psychophysical measurement methods are the method of constant stimuli, method of limits, and method of adjustment.
2. In the method of adjustment, the participant adjusts a variable comparison stimulus until it appears equal to the standard stimulus.
3. The method of constant stimuli uses multiple predetermined comparison-stimulus values rather than participant-controlled continuous adjustment.
4. In the method of limits, stimulus values are presented in ordered ascending/descending series rather than the participant freely setting the comparison stimulus.
