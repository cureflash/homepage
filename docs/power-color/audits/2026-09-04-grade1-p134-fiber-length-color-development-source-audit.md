# Grade 1 p.134 繊維の長さと発色性 — source audit

Classification: `SOURCE_BLOCKED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places `素材の知識 — 繊維` and `繊維の長さと発色性` at p.134. This establishes topic membership and order only; the public AFT material does not expose the textbook's causal rule, comparison conditions, fiber classes, thresholds, or terminology for the relationship between fiber length and color development.

## Source routes checked
The Japan Chemical Fibers Association explicitly defines `フィラメント（長繊維）` as continuous-length fiber and `ステープル（短繊維）` as short fiber cut from continuous bundles. METI/public industry material likewise establishes that fibers such as polyester exist as both filament and staple forms. These sources resolve the existence and terminology of long/short fiber forms, but they do not establish the specific p.134 AFT relationship between fiber length and `発色性`.

Exact-title/general searches also surfaced commercial cotton and textile product pages that associate longer cotton, luster, finishing, or vivid color. Those routes are rejected because product-specific claims confound fiber length with yarn fineness, weave, mercerization and other finishing conditions, and therefore cannot establish the unpublished AFT general rule.

## Rejected inference
- Do not turn `フィラメント=長繊維` / `ステープル=短繊維` into an unstated rule about which develops color better.
- Do not infer a general color-development relation from individual cotton/polyester products.
- Do not infer hue, saturation, lightness, gloss, RGB/HEX, or visual appearance from fiber length.
- Do not reconstruct the AFT explanation from generic fashion/textile study sites.

## Authoring boundary
No question authoring for `繊維の長さと発色性` until an explicit auditable authority establishes the relationship and relevant conditions closely enough to the AFT topic. The basic filament/staple terminology may be reused only when independently aligned to another resolved official heading; it does not resolve this heading itself.
