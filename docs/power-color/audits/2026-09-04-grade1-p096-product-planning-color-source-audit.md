## Classification
`SOURCE_BLOCKED`

## Result
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places p.096 `コンシューマプロダクツのビジネスプロセスと色彩 — 商品企画における色彩` immediately after p.095 `企業アイデンティティ戦略における色彩` and before the p.100 design/manufacturing items.

Current public AFT material confirms that Grade 1 covers practical color marketing/color research and the official p.096 heading/order, but it does not expose the unpublished p.096 fact inventory, product-planning process, sequence, decision criteria, deliverables, research inputs, or color-selection rules.

The Japan Color Research Institute publicly confirms that it serves people involved in color design and product development and offers color-related training/research, but that only establishes the broad relevance of color to product development. It does not determine which concrete product-planning facts, steps, or rules belong to AFT p.096. Generic product-planning or marketing sources would therefore reconstruct AFT-specific scope by inference.
