## Auditable corroborating authority

### Journal of Vision / PubMed
Kuriki (2015) defines mode of color appearance as the concept that differences in whether a medium emits, transmits, or reflects light can change color appearance. The same light may appear differently when perceived as reflected from a surface versus emitted from a light source.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26057547/

### Illuminating Engineering Institute of Japan / CiNii
A 2007 paper explicitly distinguishes reflected-surface appearance (`object color`) from self-emitting-light appearance (`light-source color`) in its discussion of color-appearance mode.
- https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390282680525571712

### Journal of Vision / PubMed
Yamauchi and Uchikawa (2005) report that mode of color appearance is not determined solely by physical stimulus properties; surrounding-stimulus/depth conditions also affect whether a stimulus is perceived in surface-color mode.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16097864/

## Authoring-safe fact families
A later p.032 authoring checkpoint may use only these narrow nonvisual relations:
1. `mode of color appearance` concerns qualitative differences in how color is perceived depending on whether light is reflected/emitted/transmitted.
2. Reflected-surface appearance and self-emitting-light appearance can be distinguished as different appearance modes.
3. Mode is not determined solely by the target stimulus; surrounding/depth context can affect the judgment.
