## Corroborating authority for Judd's four-principle framework

A Waseda University doctoral dissertation reviewing color-harmony literature states that Deane B. Judd summarized four principles guiding color-harmony theories:
- Principle of Order
- Principle of Familiarity
- Principle of Similarity
- Principle of Unambiguity

The dissertation further states that the Principle of Familiarity corresponds to natural harmony. This independently corroborates the direct AFT sample relation without requiring visual reconstruction.

Source:
- Waseda University institutional repository dissertation returned under `早稲田大学審査学位論文` (repository PDF; literature review section on color harmony).

A current DIC Color Design article also describes Judd's four principles and explicitly labels `なじみの原理 / Principle of Familiarity`; this is supplementary only and does not override AFT or the academic source.

Source:
- https://www.dic-color.com/knowledge/business/adobe_color_02.html
