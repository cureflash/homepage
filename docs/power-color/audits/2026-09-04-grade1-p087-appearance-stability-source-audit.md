# Power Color Grade 1 — p.087 ものの見えの安定性 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places `色知覚の複雑性 — ものの見えの安定性` at p.087. Public AFT material establishes heading/order only.

## Auditable corroboration used
- Morimoto, *応用物理* 93(9), 2024, `Mechanisms of human color constancy underpinning stable visual perception`: color constancy maintains stable color perception despite changes in surrounding illumination.
- University of Tokyo psychology seminar summary on illumination changes and color perception: object colors can remain nearly unchanged in appearance despite large illumination-color changes; observation conditions can affect the degree of color constancy.
- Kochi University of Technology report on peer-reviewed work: longer time to apprehend illumination and surrounding colored objects improved categorical color constancy in the reported experiment.

## Accepted fact families
1. Color constancy is the stability of perceived object color despite changes in illumination.
2. Color constancy is not absolute; its degree can depend on observation conditions.
3. In the reported categorical-color-constancy study, longer exposure to the illumination improved constancy.
4. In the same study, surrounding colored reference objects improved constancy.
