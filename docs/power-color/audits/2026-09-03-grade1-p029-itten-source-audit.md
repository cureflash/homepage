## Auditable corroborating authority

### Getty Research Institute — Bauhaus color teaching
Getty's Bauhaus research exhibition documents Johannes Itten's 1921 color star and describes it as an unfolding/flattening of Runge's sphere to show dark-light polarities. It identifies a white center, black tips, and a twelve-color equatorial zone, and states that Itten organized color study through fundamental contrast categories.

Source:
- https://www.getty.edu/research/exhibitions_events/exhibitions/bauhaus/new_artist/form_color/color/

### MIT / peer-reviewed conference paper — Itten harmony and seven contrasts
An MIT-hosted academic paper describes Itten's color-harmony concept and identifies seven kinds of color contrast: hue, light-dark, cold-warm, complementary, simultaneous, saturation, and extension. It also states that complementary colors are placed in opposite directions on Itten's hue wheel.

Source:
- https://people.csail.mit.edu/jahanian/papers/Jahanian_ColorDesign_ADoMC_EI2013.pdf
