## Auditable rule source

Accepted corroboration:
- `tokyo_landscape_color_guideline_current` — Tokyo Metropolitan Government, `東京都景観色彩ガイドライン`, current public download/index rechecked 2026-09-03, especially the application section on planning in accordance with the intent of color standards.

The Tokyo guideline explicitly states that its application section explains color planning in harmony with surrounding landscape, considering building/location conditions, viewing distance, color arrangement and material. It also states that landscape color standards include quantitative criteria while qualitative criteria require harmony with surroundings; planning should not stop at numerical compliance but should consider the purpose of the standards and the surrounding streetscape. It organizes landscape consideration by distant, middle and near views, and requires the individual location to be understood before planning.

For the accepted nonvisual boundary, the guideline further distinguishes distant/middle views as the landscape skeleton formed by natural or historical elements and near views as characteristics accumulated locally. These are planning viewpoints, not image-derived color judgments.

## Accepted micro-skill boundary

Authorize `pc2.landscape.design_considerations` only for deterministic text questions about these explicit propositions:
1. landscape color planning should consider harmony with the surrounding landscape;
2. quantitative/numerical compliance is necessary where applicable but is not by itself the planning endpoint;
3. the intent of the standards and the surrounding streetscape should also be considered;
4. individual location and relevant landscape resources should be understood before color planning;
5. planning may consider distant, middle and near views rather than only one viewing condition;
6. distant/middle views relate to the landscape skeleton formed by natural or historical elements, while near views relate to locally accumulated place characteristics;
7. viewing distance is an explicit planning factor, as are arrangement and material, without inferring their visual outcomes.

## Exclusions

This audit does not authorize:
- Tokyo-specific Munsell thresholds, hue/chroma/value limits or district-specific numerical criteria as universal Grade 2 facts;
- any RGB/HEX/display value, monitor-facing swatch, screenshot or sampled-image answer;
- any claim about how a particular material must look or which hue/tone must be chosen;
- any unstated viewing-distance geometry, field-of-view calculation or photograph-derived judgment;
- any universal ban on distinctive colors;
- any inference that numerical compliance alone guarantees good landscape color planning.

The source contract is conceptual and relational only.
