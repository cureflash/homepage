# Power Color Grade 2 p.128 `和色名` source audit

Checked: 2026-09-03

## Result

`和色名` individual-name authoring is source-blocked.

The current Color Certification Association Grade 2 official table of contents explicitly places `慣用色名（JISの慣用色名より）` at p.128, with `和色名` at p.128 and `外来色名` at p.132. This confirms scope and order only; the public TOC does not expose the individual Grade 2 `和色名` set or readings.

Current Japan Color Research Institute material for the JIS color-name book confirms that the product is based on JIS Z 8102:2001 and that conventional color names are indexed in the accompanying explanatory material, but the public product page does not expose the Color Certification Association Grade 2 p.128 subset.

Public secondary lists can enumerate the full JIS conventional Japanese-name corpus, including readings and Munsell values, but that does not establish which individual names the current Grade 2 official text selects on pp.128–131. Grade 3 membership must not be projected into Grade 2.

## Accepted facts

- Current AFT Grade 2 official scope/order: `和色名` p.128 followed by `外来色名` p.132.
- JIS Z 8102:2001 is the governing JIS conventional-color-name framework referenced by the current Grade 2 heading.
- The JIS color-name product maintained by JCRI/JSA contains conventional color names and an index, but its public product page does not expose the Grade 2 textbook subset.

## Not authorized

Do not author questions that require any of the following until a current explicit auditable Grade 2 source exposes them:

- individual p.128–131 `和色名` membership;
- readings tied specifically to the Grade 2 subset;
- Munsell values tied to the Grade 2 subset;
- etymology or historical explanations;
- RGB/HEX/display values, swatches, image appearance, or monitor judgments;
- Grade 3 → Grade 2 membership inference;
- using the full 147-name JIS Japanese corpus as if it were the Grade 2 p.128 list.
