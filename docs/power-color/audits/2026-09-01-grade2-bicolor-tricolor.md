# Grade 2 bicolor / tricolor source audit

Checked: 2026-09-01

## Scope authority

The current Color Certification Association Grade 2 official text table of contents places bicolor / tricolor in the Grade 2 color-harmony sequence after camaieu / faux-camaieu. The TOC is used for scope/order only.

## Explicit rule sources

### Plus Colors
`https://pluscolors.net/color_knowledge/bicolore-tricolore/`

The article is supervised by a Color Certification Level 1 coordinator and cites the official 2020 revised Color Certification text. It explicitly defines:
- bicolore: a clear, contrastive combination of two colors; also called bicolor;
- tricolore: a combination of three colors with clear contrast in hue or tone.

### MEGASOFT / 河村容治
`https://www.megasoft.co.jp/3d/colorscheme/pattern4.php`

The educational page is authored by former Tokyo City University professor 河村容治 (PhD in art, first-class architect, Japan Society for Interior Studies director). It independently defines:
- bicolore: two-color scheme with clear contrast;
- tricolore: three-color scheme with clear contrast.

## Retained intersection

Power Color may encode only the independently shared nonvisual rule:
- bicolor / bicolore = a clear-contrast two-color scheme;
- tricolor / tricolore = a clear-contrast three-color scheme;
- bicolore may also be called bicolor.

Do not encode the sources' additional example-dependent claims as mandatory rules. In particular, do not infer or require exact hue differences, PCCS tone boundaries, saturation thresholds, achromatic placement, RGB/HEX, monitor values, or image appearance.

This retained intersection is sufficient for text-only definition/count/distinction questions and does not authorize monitor-facing questions.
