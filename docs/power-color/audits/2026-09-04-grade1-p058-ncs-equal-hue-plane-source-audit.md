## Accepted authoring boundary
1. One NCS Colour Triangle is defined for each hue.
2. A colour's position in that triangle defines its nuance.
3. Neutral white/black/grey colours lie along the vertical side of the NCS Colour Triangle.
4. In the example nuance `1040`, the first two digits mean 10% blackness and the last two mean 40% chromaticness.
