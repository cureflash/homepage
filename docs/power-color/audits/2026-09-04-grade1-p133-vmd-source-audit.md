# Grade 1 p.133 VMD — source audit

Classification: `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places `VMD` at p.133 under `ファッションビジネスにおけるカラーコーディネーターの役割`. The same official TOC separately places `ビジュアルマーチャンダイジングと色彩` at p.136, so this audit deliberately limits p.133 to the basic VMD concept and does not import the later p.136-p.137 detailed colour-coordination content.

## Accepted corroboration
The Japan Visual Merchandising Association (JAVMA), the professional association supporting the national `商品装飾展示技能検定`, defines visual merchandising as a retail strategic activity within marketing. It describes VMD as integrating merchandising with the visual expression of commercial space from the customer's standpoint to provide a sales environment that is easy to see, choose and buy from while communicating company/brand identity and promotional concepts.

Sugino Fashion College independently describes VMD as visual spatial presentation in shops/event spaces using showcases, racks and related fixtures while considering brand concept, seasonality and trends, with the purpose of visually conveying brand worldview and product appeal.

These sources support only the basic nonvisual VMD concept: VMD is broader than decorative display alone, is linked to merchandising/retail strategy, uses commercial-space presentation, and communicates brand/product value visually. They do not establish the unpublished AFT-specific method, implementation sequence, VP/PP/IP taxonomy, layout rules, colour-arrangement rules, or later p.136-p.137 treatment.

## Rejected routes
- Do not reconstruct the p.133 textbook text from generic VMD study sites or private consultants.
- Do not import p.136 `ビジュアルマーチャンダイジング（VMD）の取り組み` procedures into p.133.
- Do not infer display geometry, customer-flow rules, colour arrangements, RGB/HEX, or visual judgments.

## Authoring boundary
Text-only questions may test the narrow corroborated facts that VMD is a visual-merchandising retail/marketing activity, integrates merchandising with the visual expression of sales space, aims at a customer-friendly attractive selling environment, and communicates brand/product concepts visually. Detailed operational procedure remains out of scope.
