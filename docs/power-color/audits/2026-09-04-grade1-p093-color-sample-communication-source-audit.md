# Power Color Grade 1 — p.093 カラーコミュニケーションツールの役割 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official TOC fixes the p.093 subheadings as `色指定ツールとしての色見本`, `色づくりの参照ツールとしての色見本`, and `色見本の種類`. Public AFT material does not expose the textbook body, so this audit does not reconstruct an exhaustive AFT taxonomy.

## Accepted corroborating boundary
Use only system-owner facts explicitly published by DIC about professional color guides and charts:

1. DIC states that its color guide is used in design work when selecting and communicating colors.
2. DIC states that the DIC Color Guide series is used broadly in manufacturing as a color-specification tool.
3. DIC states that its Process Color Note IJ List shows spot-color and process-color reproductions side by side and supports color specification and client color confirmation.
4. DIC states that its offset-printing color chart is used for color specification and quality control.

These facts support a narrow nonvisual p.093 slice about the role of physical/system color samples as professional color-communication, specification, comparison, and control tools. They do not establish the unpublished AFT list of all color-sample types or any AFT-specific classification labels.

## Accepted sources
- AFT current official Grade 1 TOC: official p.093 heading/order only.
- DIC `デザインの現場に！ ～DICカラーガイド`: selection/communication role.

- DIC 2025 Process Color Note IJ List release: side-by-side spot/process comparison, color specification, client confirmation.
- DIC 2025 Color Chart release: color specification and quality-control use in offset printing.

## Excluded
- Exact unpublished AFT definitions, wording, taxonomy, counts, or examples.
- Any color appearance judgment, swatch recognition, RGB/HEX/display values, or monitor-facing question.
- Claims that DIC product categories equal the complete AFT `色見本の種類` taxonomy.
