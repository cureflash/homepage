## Auditable authority used
- AFT current official Grade 1 textbook table of contents: establishes the exact heading/order only. https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf
- Liberini et al., *Color Research & Application* (2023), `Munsell and Ostwald colour spaces: A comparison in the field of hair colouring`: states that for each Ostwald pure colour a triangle connects that pure colour to white and black; the white-black side lies on the neutral gray axis; the top series connects pure white to pure colour and is the light clear series; the bottom series connects pure black to pure colour and is the dark clear series. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/col.22818
- Pohlmann, *ChemTexts* 6, 9 (2020), `Measures and numbers for colors. The color system of Wilhelm Ostwald`: peer-reviewed lecture text on Ostwald's colour triangle and color-solid organization. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40828-020-0104-5

## Accepted authoring boundary
Only these deterministic nonvisual facts are source-resolved enough to author:
1. For each Ostwald pure colour, the corresponding triangular section connects that pure colour with white and black.
2. The white-black side of that triangle belongs to the neutral gray axis.
3. The boundary series from pure white to the pure colour is the light clear series and contains white + pure colour without black.
4. The boundary series from pure black to the pure colour is the dark clear series and contains black + pure colour without white.

These facts describe structural relations only. They do not authorize reconstructing the exact AFT p.054 drawing.
