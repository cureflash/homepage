# Grade 1 p.073 色彩心理 — 心理学的尺度構成法 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places p.073 `心理学的尺度構成法` after p.072 `心理学的尺度構成法の基本` and before p.080 `心理物理学的測定法`.

AFT's current official seminar page for the Grade 1 textbook explicitly states that pp.72–79 `心理学的尺度構成法` cover the four scale levels through methods including `一対比較法` and `SD法`. This resolves those two methods as in-scope for the current Grade 1 psychology section, but does not expose the unpublished textbook procedure details or every method covered on p.073–79.

## Accepted corroborating authority
- Keio University psychology laboratory's paired-comparison support material states that paired comparison evaluates two materials as a pair and indirectly constructs psychological distance, with a one-dimensional target characteristic assumed.
- The University of Electro-Communications describes an actual SD-method study using adjective-pair scales and a five-step subjective evaluation to quantify an atmosphere/image.
- CiNii-indexed university psychology research independently documents SD-method impression evaluation using adjective-pair scales.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. Paired comparison evaluates stimuli/materials two at a time as pairs rather than requiring a single absolute judgment in isolation.
2. Paired-comparison data can be used to indirectly construct a one-dimensional psychological scale/distance for the evaluated characteristic.
3. The SD (Semantic Differential) method evaluates impression/image using opposing adjective-pair scales.
4. An SD evaluation can use graded multi-step ratings between adjective-pair endpoints; the accepted public example uses a five-step scale.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not reconstruct unpublished AFT method inventory, exact page-level allocation within pp.73–79, exact questionnaires, item counts, adjective lists, statistical algorithms, Thurstone/Scheffé formulas, scale-conversion procedures, factor-analysis procedures, numerical worked examples, diagrams, RGB/HEX/display values, or monitor-facing judgments. Do not infer that the external example's five-step count is the only AFT-approved SD format.

## Decision
Authoring is allowed only for the four deterministic nonvisual method facts above. Keep the classification `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`; the external sources corroborate method definitions while AFT first-party material establishes current Grade 1 scope and explicitly names paired comparison and SD only at the pp.72–79 section level.
