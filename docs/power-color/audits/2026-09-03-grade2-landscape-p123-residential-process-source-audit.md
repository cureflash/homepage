# Grade 2 Landscape p.123 `住宅の色彩設計プロセス` — source audit

Checked: 2026-09-03

## Result

Source-resolved for a narrow deterministic nonvisual process boundary.

## Primary authority

Current Color Certification Association Grade 2 table of contents (`https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-2st-grade_mokuji.pdf`) places, under `生活環境における景観色彩設計`:

1. `景観色彩設計の配慮事項` p.122
2. `住宅の色彩設計プロセス` p.123
3. `色を測ることによる周辺環境の把握` p.123
4. `配色計画` p.124
5. `色彩管理` p.125

The public table of contents establishes current Grade 2 scope/order but does not publish the full official-text prose for the p.123 process.

## Auditable professional corroboration

The Japan Paint Manufacturers Association current 2025 CCS practical color-coordinator seminar explicitly teaches `色彩設計のプロセス(手順)` and includes a `戸建住宅` color-design exercise. Its published practical work items explicitly include:

- actual surrounding-environment color survey (`視感測色`);
- discussion to consolidate a color-design concept;
- creation of a color scheme based on that concept;
- creation of a color-design proposal document;
- creation of a CG for the color scheme;
- presentation of the color-design proposal and instructor evaluation.

Source: `https://www.toryo.or.jp/jp/event/seminar/CCS2025ad.html`.

## Accepted authoring boundary

A Grade 2 text-only micro-skill may test only these explicit structural/process facts:

- current official scope includes a residential color-design process;
- the official scope places surrounding-environment measurement, color planning, and color management directly after the residential-process heading;
- a current professional practical process explicitly includes a detached-house exercise and surrounding-environment visual color survey;
- a color-design concept is consolidated from the exercise work;
- the color scheme is explicitly developed based on that concept;
- proposal-document/CG/presentation activities are explicit downstream artifacts/actions in the corroborating practical process.

This boundary is relational and nonvisual. The public JPMA bullet list is not treated as proof of a universal mandatory total order except where a relation is explicitly stated (`コンセプトに基づいた色彩設計`). The AFT table-of-contents order may be asked only as official scope/order, not as a claim that every real project must follow that exact sequence.

## Prohibited inference

Do not infer or generate:

- RGB/HEX/display values or monitor swatches;
- fixed hue/tone/Munsell recommendations;
- answers from photographs, CG appearance, material appearance, or unstated geometry;
- numerical measurement values not explicitly published;
- a universal project workflow stronger than the explicit source wording;
- detailed content of `色彩管理` beyond its confirmed inclusion/order in current official scope.

## Decision

Authorize pending-first authoring for `pc2.landscape.residential_color_design_process` using only the accepted facts above. Independently re-answer every item before promotion. Grade 3 source/display gates remain unchanged.
