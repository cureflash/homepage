# Grade 2 Landscape p.117 source audit — 景観をつくる要素

Checked: 2026-09-02

## Scope authority

Current Color Certification Association Grade 2 official text table of contents:
- https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-2st-grade_mokuji.pdf
- p.117 explicitly lists `景観をつくる要素` immediately after the p.116 landscape/public-nature material.

The official table of contents is used for scope/order only; it does not by itself authorize unstated detailed facts.

## Auditable content authority

Mutsu City official Landscape Plan page:
- https://www.city.mutsu.lg.jp/kurashi/machi/keikan/mutsushikeikankeikaku.html

The page explicitly states that elements composing landscape are diverse and gives examples including:
- 建築物
- 工作物
- 道路
- 公園
- 河川
- 港湾
- 屋外広告物
- 美化活動
- 山
- 森林
- 公共事業
- 個人事業

This is sufficient for a narrow nonvisual Grade 2 boundary about the breadth and explicit examples of landscape-composing elements. It also establishes, without image inference, that the concept is not limited to one class of physical facility or only to public projects.

## Accepted authoring boundary

`pc2.landscape.element_examples` may test only:
1. the explicit examples listed above;
2. the fact that landscape-composing elements are diverse rather than limited to buildings or public facilities alone;
3. the explicit inclusion of `美化活動`, showing that the listed landscape elements are not restricted to static physical objects;
4. the explicit inclusion of both `公共事業` and `個人事業`.

Questions must be text-only. When a distractor is not in the source list, wording must ask about what the cited source explicitly lists or about a restriction contradicted by the explicit list; do not claim that an unlisted item can never be a landscape element in every context.

## Exclusions

Do not infer or author:
- color, hue, tone, material, texture, shape or building-form properties;
- RGB/HEX or monitor-facing values;
- answers from photographs, screenshots, maps or unstated geometry;
- visual area ratios or prominence;
- a universal closed taxonomy of all possible landscape elements;
- natural/artificial category labels unless a later authority explicitly fixes those labels for the Grade 2 text boundary;
- p.117 `景観色彩における色彩設計の役割`, which requires a separate source audit.

Grade 3 remains incomplete and all existing Grade 3 source/display blockers remain unchanged.
