## Auditable authority used
Current CIE authority only:

- CIE S 017:2020 e-ILV `illuminant`: radiation with a relative spectral power distribution defined over the wavelength range that influences object-colour perception.
- ISO/CIE 11664-2:2022 `Colorimetry — Part 2: CIE Standard Illuminants`: current standard defining CIE standard illuminants A and D65 and adding D50 as a CIE standard illuminant.
- CIE standard illuminant A: intended to represent typical tungsten-filament lighting; relative SPD corresponds to a Planckian radiator at approximately 2856 K.
- CIE standard illuminant D65: intended to represent average daylight with correlated colour temperature approximately 6500 K.
- CIE standard illuminant D50: intended to represent daylight with correlated colour temperature approximately 5000 K.
