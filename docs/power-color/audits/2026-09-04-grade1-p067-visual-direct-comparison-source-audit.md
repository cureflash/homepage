# Grade 1 p.067 測色 — 視感測色―直接比較法 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official material places p.067 `視感測色―直接比較法` after p.066 `測色とは` and before p.069 `物理測色`. AFT public material establishes heading/order only.

## Accepted corroborating authority
- ISO 3668:2017 `Paints and varnishes — Visual comparison of colour of paints`, current confirmed International Standard. Its public abstract specifies visual comparison of a specimen colour against a standard, identifies the comparison standard as either a reference standard or a freshly prepared standard, and requires artificial light sources in a standard booth.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. Direct visual colour comparison compares a specimen colour against a standard.
2. The comparison standard can be a reference standard or a freshly prepared standard.
3. ISO 3668:2017 specifies artificial light sources for the visual comparison method.
4. The specified comparison is carried out in a standard booth.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not reconstruct unpublished AFT procedures, observation geometry, adaptation time, illuminance, exact lamp specifications, sample dimensions, arrangement diagrams, tolerances, special-effect-pigment handling beyond the public ISO abstract, RGB/HEX/display values, or monitor-facing judgments. Do not generalize coating-specific details beyond the four explicit comparison facts above.

## Decision
Authoring is allowed only for the four deterministic nonvisual fact families above, with ISO 3668:2017 attribution where needed.
