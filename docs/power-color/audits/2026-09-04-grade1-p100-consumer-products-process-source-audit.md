# Power Color Grade 1 — p.100 consumer-products process source audit

Checked: 2026-09-04

## Official scope
Current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places three p.100 items under `コンシューマプロダクツのビジネスプロセスと色彩`: `商品デザインにおける色彩`, `製造における色彩`, and `流通における色彩`. This establishes membership, labels, page, and sequence only.

## Routes checked
- Current AFT Grade 1 scope / official textbook page: confirms Grade 1 includes practical color-marketing / color-research methods, but does not expose the p.100 body.
- Current AFT Grade 1 table of contents: fixes the three p.100 headings and their sequence.
- Current AFT Grade 1 first-stage sample questions: exposes authoritative facts for later `カラーマーケティング`, `カラーリサーチ`, and `CMF®` topics, but contains no `商品デザイン`, `製造`, or `流通` p.100 rule/process fact.
- Japan Design Promotion Institute public material: supports only broad statements about design and relationships among people/society/products; it does not establish AFT's p.100 color process, sequence, roles, deliverables, or decision rules.
- Japan Color Research Institute public product/color-tool material: supports broad use of color samples/charts in design and color planning, but does not establish the unpublished AFT p.100 process.

## Classification
- p.100 `商品デザインにおける色彩`: `SOURCE_BLOCKED`
- p.100 `製造における色彩`: `SOURCE_BLOCKED`
- p.100 `流通における色彩`: `SOURCE_BLOCKED`

## Why blocked
The accepted public evidence establishes only topic membership and broad relevance. It does not expose a closed deterministic AFT fact set for how color is handled at product-design, manufacturing, or distribution stages. Generic industrial-design, manufacturing, logistics, or product-development practice would invent the AFT-specific business-process boundary and is therefore rejected.

## Authoring decision
No authoring. Do not infer process order, responsible roles, handoff artifacts, tolerances, color-management procedure, production constraints, distribution constraints, or visual/color judgments from generic practice. Do not repeat this route without new explicit AFT or equally direct authoritative evidence tied to these p.100 headings.
