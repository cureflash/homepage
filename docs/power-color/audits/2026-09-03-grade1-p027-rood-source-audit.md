## Auditable corroborating authority

### Metropolitan Museum of Art — Rood primary-work record
The Met's Thomas J. Watson Library catalogue records Ogden Nicholas Rood's `Modern chromatics : with applications to art and industry`, published in 1879. This fixes the work/author/date without relying on a study-summary site.

Source:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/738466

### University of Chicago academic literature — optical mixture
A University of Chicago academic article discussing Rood's theory quotes/describes his account of optical mixing: different colours placed side by side in lines or dots can, at sufficient viewing distance, blend in the eye/retina and produce a new colour sensation. This supports a narrow nonvisual relation between Rood and optical mixture without reconstructing an AFT diagram.

Source:
- https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/699818

### Metropolitan Museum of Art — complementary-colour intensification context
The Met's discussion of nineteenth-century colour theory identifies Chevreul and Rood as major theoretical influences and states the shared practical message that opposite hues on the colour wheel, when layered or placed adjacent, are intensified in richness. This may be used only as corroborated historical/theoretical context; it does not establish the exact unpublished AFT p.027 wording or category system.

Source:
- https://www.metmuseum.org/ko/perspectives/mary-cassatt-modernist-mother-child
