# Grade 2 Interior p.105 source audit

Checked: 2026-09-02

## Scope authority

Current Color Certification Association Grade 2 materials keep Interior as an active Grade 2 application domain. The current official Grade 2 table of contents places `インテリア色彩の考え方` and `インテリア空間の分類とゾーニング` at p.105.

## Accepted auditable secondary authority

Cleverly Home Fukushima, `知っておきたい！インテリアコーディネートの基本知識ー色彩編ー` (2023-10-10), explicitly uses the same section headings and cites `2019．文部科学省後援 色彩検定 公式テキスト 2級編` as its reference.

Accepted nonvisual propositions from that page:

### インテリア色彩の考え方
1. 生活を包む色彩であること — interior color continuously affects occupants.
2. 目的や用途に応じた色彩であること — rooms differ in role/function, so color planning should fit purpose/use.
3. 住むための色彩であること — finishes are not easily changed after construction; durability of preference and maintainability matter.
4. 利用者に応じた色彩であること — planning should account for who mainly uses the space.

### インテリア空間の分類とゾーニング
Residential spaces are classified by principal life activity into three zones:
- パブリックゾーン: shared by family and visitors/many people.
- プライベートゾーン: used by a specific individual within the family.
- サービスゾーン: functional housework and water-use/wet-area spaces.

DAIKEN current professional housing guidance independently corroborates zoning as grouping rooms with similar uses and arranging those groups as units. This supports the zoning concept without adding a fourth Color Certification zone.

## Exclusions

Do not infer from floor-plan geometry, photographs, room color appearance, RGB/HEX, sampled pixels, unstated area ratios, or remembered textbook examples. Do not broaden the accepted three-zone taxonomy with `移動ゾーン` from unrelated taxonomies. Do not convert later room-specific color recommendations into universal p.105 rules unless separately source-audited.
