# Grade 2 source audit — ファッションの分類

Checked: 2026-09-02

## Scope authority

Current Color Certification Association material remains the primary authority.

- Current Grade 2 level page links the current official Grade 2 text table of contents and describes Grade 2 as applying color knowledge to professional fields including visual design and related coordination.
- Current official Grade 2 text TOC places `ファッションの分類` at p.091, immediately after `ファッションカラーの分類` p.090 and before `ファッションの配色` p.095.
- Current official Grade 2 sample confirms fashion color coordination is within the Grade 2 applied scope.

Primary references:

## Rule audit

The reviewed public first-party material establishes the topic name and order, but does not expose the detailed classification rule used on p.091.

Secondary search located current Grade 2 preparation material that independently names `ファッションの分類` as a lesson/topic, but the publicly accessible text does not expose a sufficiently explicit, auditable classification mapping that can be converted into deterministic Grade 2 text-choice answers without importing unstated textbook content.

Examples reviewed for scope corroboration only:
- OnSuku Grade 2 course outline: `8-1. ファッションの分類`.
- Current 2026 Grade 2 preparation summaries describing fashion as an examined application field.

These sources do not justify inventing category membership, silhouettes, garment-style mappings, personal-color mappings, seasonal-image mappings, or other detailed rules that are not explicitly stated in accepted authority.

## Decision

`ファッションの分類` p.091 is source-blocked for Power Color authoring at this checkpoint.

Do not generate a `pc2.fashion.*` micro-skill from the TOC label alone. In particular, do not infer answers from fashion photographs, silhouettes, garment appearance, current trend examples, RGB/HEX, swatches, sampled pixels, or unstated style geometry.

The previous `pc2.fashion.basic_trend_color_classification` p.090 slice remains valid and promoted because its narrow basic/trend rule boundary was separately sourced and independently QA'd. This audit does not broaden or alter that skill.

## Exact continuation

Skip p.091 authoring unless a future explicit auditable rule source is acquired. Continue source acquisition in official-text order at `ファッションの配色` p.095. Author nothing there until a separate explicit nonvisual answer rule is fixed.
