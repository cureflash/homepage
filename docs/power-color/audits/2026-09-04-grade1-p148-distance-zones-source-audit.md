# Grade 1 p.148 遠景・中景・近景とは — Source Audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope
AFT current official Grade 1 textbook table of contents explicitly places `遠景・中景・近景とは` at p.148 under `遠景・中景・近景を踏まえた色彩計画`. This establishes topic membership and order only; unpublished AFT body wording is not reconstructed.

## Accepted authority
1. AFT current official Grade 1 textbook table of contents: https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf
2. 新宿区 `景観まちづくり計画 景観形成ガイドライン` 用語集: https://www.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/shinjuku_keikanguidline/pageindices/index380.html
3. 国立研究開発法人 森林研究・整備機構 森林総合研究所 `農山村景観の景観美を環境情報から予測分析、近景の扱いで美しさアップ` (2026-03-26): https://www.ffpri.go.jp/research/saizensen/2026/20260326-2.html

Shinjuku Ward's official glossary states that 遠景・中景・近景 are terms expressing approximate landscape distance; 遠景 includes distant landscape such as ridgelines/terrain outlines and landscape seen against the sky, while 近景 includes nearby visible building form and shape. FFPRI independently treats landscape as distance zones and explicitly states that visible elements differ according to viewing distance.

## Authoring boundary
Author only text-only questions testing the following narrow public facts:
- 遠景・中景・近景 classify landscape by approximate viewing distance / distance zone.
- 遠景 concerns distant landscape, including ridgelines/terrain outlines and distant objects seen against the sky in the Shinjuku glossary.
- 近景 concerns nearby visible form/shape such as buildings in the Shinjuku glossary.
- What can be visually recognized differs with viewing distance, so distance zones are analytically distinct in FFPRI's landscape research.

Do not infer:
- unpublished AFT distance thresholds or exact definitions;
- universal numerical cutoffs from any one municipality or research study;
- color prescriptions, hue/value/chroma rules, RGB/HEX/display values, or monitor-facing judgments;
- answers from photographs, diagrams, apparent perspective, or unstated geometry.

## Rejected routes
Unsourced qualification summaries and private landscape/design blogs are not authority. Research-specific numerical distance bands are not generalized into AFT definitions.

## Decision
The p.148 heading is not directly resolved by public AFT body text, but the core nonvisual concept is corroborated by current official municipal guidance plus a current national research institute source. The topic is therefore `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`, sufficient only for a narrow four-record text-choice batch.
