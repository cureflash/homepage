## Current authoritative rule source

Accepted public-sector authority:
- `tokyo_landscape_color_guideline_2026` — Tokyo Metropolitan Government, `景観計画／東京都景観色彩ガイドライン／景観協定`, current page verified 2026-09-03.

The Tokyo Metropolitan Government explicitly explains that its landscape color guideline:
- explains color standards under the Tokyo landscape plan;
- uses numerical standards by area and target;
- sets those standards while considering surrounding landscape characteristics and the scale of the target;
- uses the standards to suppress colors that would create extremely strong discordance with the surrounding landscape;
- requires careful color planning incorporating harmony with surroundings to derive colors appropriate to a site;
- provides an application section for landscape-harmony thinking and color-planning procedures according to place and scale;
- states that numerical compliance alone is not the endpoint: the intent of the standards and harmony with the surrounding streetscape must also be considered.

This supplies a deterministic, nonvisual role boundary that is directly relevant to the official Grade 2 p.117 heading.

## Accepted micro-skill boundary

Authorize `pc2.landscape.color_design_harmony_role` only for text questions about the following explicit propositions:
1. landscape color standards provide a framework for color planning rather than permitting arbitrary isolated color selection;
2. surrounding landscape characteristics and target scale are planning considerations;
3. one role of numerical standards is to suppress colors with extreme discordance from surrounding landscape;
4. deriving site-appropriate color requires considering harmony with surroundings;
5. satisfying numerical standards alone is insufficient as a planning principle; the standards' purpose and surrounding-streetscape harmony also matter;
6. place and scale are relevant to the process of considering landscape harmony and color planning.
