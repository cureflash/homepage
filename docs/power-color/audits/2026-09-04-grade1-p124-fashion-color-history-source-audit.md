# Grade 1 p.124 ファッションカラーの変遷 — source audit

Classification: `SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope
The current AFT Grade 1 official table of contents places `ファッションカラーの変遷` at p.124, immediately after the CMF® section and before `ファッションビジネスにおけるカラーコーディネーターの役割` at p.129. The public AFT material fixes the heading/order but does not publish the p.124 body text or a closed decade-by-decade keyword list.

## Accepted corroboration
Use only narrow, auditable historical classification facts independently published by the system-owner/history source below; do not claim that every accepted fact reproduces unpublished AFT wording.

- JAFCA, `日本のファッションカラー100 流行色とファッショントレンド1945-2013`: JAFCA states that the work surveys Japanese fashion/trend-color change from 1945 to 2013 and classifies the history into eight color-movement stages: `カラーの黎明時代`, `高彩度カラー志向の時代`, `自然色志向の時代`, `ハイコントラストの時代`, `カラーコーディネートの時代`, `カラフル化と質感の時代`, `癒しの色の時代`, `光の色の時代`.
- The publisher BNN exposes the JAFCA-authored table of contents, allowing exact stage-to-keyword relations to be audited without inferring colors from images. Examples accepted for this batch are: `アメリカンルック`→stage I, `サイケデリック`→stage II, `ヒッピー`→stage III, `モノトーンブーム`→stage IV, `エコロジーカラー`→stage V, `アシッドカラー`→stage VI, `癒しの白`→stage VII, `ネオンカラー`→stage VIII.
- Showa-kan Digital Archive independently catalogs the same JAFCA book and exposes the early-stage contents through stage III, corroborating the publication identity and the initial stage/keyword sequence.

## Rejected routes
- Do not use personal study blogs, cram-school handouts, or unsourced decade summaries as authority.
- Do not infer RGB/HEX, hue/tone, exact appearance, or decade labels from images.
- Do not assume the JAFCA eight-stage model is identical to unpublished AFT prose; it is used only as strong corroboration for deterministic fashion-color-history relations under the official p.124 heading.
