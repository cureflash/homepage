# Grade 1 p.146 色相、明度、彩度と景観形成とのかかわり — Source Audit

## Classification
`SOURCE_RESOLVED`

## Official scope
AFT current official Grade 1 textbook table of contents explicitly places `色相、明度、彩度と景観形成とのかかわり` at p.146 under 景観色彩. This establishes topic membership and order only; unpublished AFT body wording is not reconstructed.

## Accepted authority
1. AFT current official Grade 1 textbook table of contents: https://www.aft.or.jp/images/text_of-1st-grade_mokuji.pdf
2. 国土交通省 道路附属物等の色彩検討に関する資料「色彩の基礎知識」: https://www.mlit.go.jp/road/ir/ir-council/road_design/pdf/guidelines.pdf

The MLIT material explicitly states the landscape-design relevance of the three color attributes and gives the following narrow deterministic relations:
- 色相: R〜Y are generally warm colors and can create warmth/liveliness; G〜B are generally cool colors and are often used to create a calm atmosphere.
- 明度: for otherwise identical objects, brighter colors tend to feel lighter and darker colors tend to feel heavier.
- 彩度: more vivid colors have higher chroma; vivid colors generally have higher conspicuity and attract attention more readily.
- The three attributes can be examined to consider an appropriate color direction for road structures.

## Decision
The p.146 topic is source-resolved only for the narrow nonvisual relation between hue/value/chroma and the explicit landscape impressions/conspicuity stated by MLIT. This is sufficient for a four-record authoring batch.
