# Grade 1 p.069 測色 — 物理測色 source audit

## Classification
`SECONDARY_CORROBORATION_REQUIRED`

## Official scope/order
Current AFT Grade 1 official textbook table of contents places p.069 `物理測色` after p.067 `視感測色―直接比較法` and before p.072 `心理学的尺度構成法の基本`. AFT public material establishes heading/order only.

## Accepted corroborating authority
- NIST `Spectrophotometry`: defines spectrophotometry as quantitative measurement of reflectance and transmittance of optical radiation and describes illumination of a material followed by detection of its optical response.
- Yoshi Ohno (NIST), `CIE Fundamentals for Color Measurements`: reviews practical colour measurement using spectroreflectometers, spectroradiometers, and tristimulus colorimeters; it distinguishes object-colour measurement from light-source colour measurement and discusses calibration/verification of instrument accuracy.

## Source-resolved fact families
1. Spectrophotometry quantitatively measures optical reflectance and transmittance.
2. Spectrophotometric measurement illuminates a material and detects the light response from that material.
3. Practical CIE colour measurement uses spectroreflectometers for object-colour measurement and spectroradiometers for light-source colour measurement.
4. Tristimulus colorimeters are also practical instruments used for colour measurement.

## Explicit exclusions
Do not reconstruct unpublished AFT device taxonomy, optical geometry, integration-sphere arrangements, wavelength intervals, formulas, calibration steps, exact instrument diagrams, instrument-selection rules, tolerances, numerical tables, RGB/HEX/display values, or monitor-facing judgments. Do not infer exact AFT wording from generic instrument manuals.

## Decision
Authoring is allowed only for the four deterministic nonvisual fact families above. This does not assert exhaustive coverage of the unpublished p.069 textbook body.
