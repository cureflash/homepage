## p.082 記憶色と色記憶 accepted corroborating boundary
Audit: `docs/power-color/audits/2026-09-04-grade1-p082-memory-color-source-audit.md`.

Dedicated gate: `qualifications/color-certification/tests/grade1-memory-color-p082-authoring.test.js`.
Accepted facts are limited to four nonvisual research-backed relations: familiar-object typical colour can modulate perceived colour; Hansen fruit achromatic settings shifted opposite the typical colour; Bartleson familiar-object memory colours differed from corresponding natural colours and tended toward the dominant chromatic attribute; Pérez-Carpinell memory matching generally showed larger mean colour differences than simultaneous matching and a significant delay-time effect. Exact unpublished AFT definitions/examples, colour coordinates, RGB/HEX/display values, swatches, and universal stimulus-independent memory-colour claims remain excluded.
