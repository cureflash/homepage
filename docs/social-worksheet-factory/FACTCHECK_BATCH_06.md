## Independent evidence review

The current Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology high-school Geography/History commentary was independently reopened for D(1) through D(4). Generated wording was not treated as evidence.

D(1) was aligned to MEXT's actual inquiry examples: Cold War and international relations, movement of people and capital, advanced information communications, food and population, resources/energy and the global environment, infectious disease, and coexistence of diverse people.

D(2) was aligned to MEXT's treatment of decolonization and Asian/African states, regional conflicts under the Cold War, military expansion and nuclear-arms control, regional cooperation in Western Europe and Southeast Asia, planned economies, and Japan's high economic growth.

D(3) was aligned to MEXT's two explicit knowledge strands. The market-economy strand covers the oil crises, economic development in Asian regions, market opening and economic liberalization, and information/communications technology. The international-politics strand covers the end of the Cold War, democratization, expansion/transformation of regional integration, and the spread of regional conflicts and responses to them.

D(4) was corrected from a generic summary into the required History Comprehensive capstone: with a sustainable society in view, students themselves set a theme, use sources, compare and relate Japan with other countries/regions, and investigate a contemporary issue based on its historical background.

## Material corrections

- `globalization-questions`: replaced a narrow Cold War / decolonization / ICT summary with the full MEXT D(1) inquiry perspective set.
- `cold-war-decolonization`: expanded the unit to decolonization, nation-building, regional conflicts, military expansion and nuclear-arms management rather than describing the Cold War only as US-Soviet confrontation.
- `high-growth-order-change`: aligned the second D(2) strand to Western European / Southeast Asian regional cooperation, planned economies, Japanese high economic growth and associated social/environmental change.
- `cold-war-end-regional-integration`: aligned the international-politics strand to Cold War termination, democratization, regional integration, diversified causes of regional conflicts, and responses including UN/PKO activity.
- `market-information-globalization`: added the oil crises, Asian economic development, market opening/liberalization and the 1990s information-communications expansion required by MEXT; removed the generation-stage oversimplification that reduced the unit to cross-border flows alone.
- `contemporary-issues-outlook`: converted the unit into a genuine inquiry capstone. It no longer presents a fixed list of modern issues as the answer; students set a theme and examine background, causes, results and effects using historical evidence.
