## Independent evidence review

The generated wording was not treated as evidence. Curriculum placement and required treatment were independently reopened against the current Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology commentary for high-school Geography/History (`高等学校学習指導要領（平成30年告示）解説 地理歴史編`).

The MEXT commentary confirms the structure and intent of A「歴史の扉」, B(1)「近代化への問い」, B(2)「結び付く世界と日本の開国」, B(3)「国民国家と明治維新」, and B(4)「近代化と現代的な諸課題」. It also supplies the factual treatment used here for 18th-century Asian production/distribution, British industrialization, transport/communications, world-market formation, the expansion of industrialized Western states into Asia, nation-state formation, and the historical/current-issues framework.

Specific historical claims were separately checked against:

- アジア歴史資料センター「南京条約」 — 1842 treaty and five treaty ports.
- 国立国会図書館「開国―近代の幕開け」「日米和親条約写」 — Perry chronology and the Shimoda/Hakodate provisions.
- 国立公文書館「廃藩置県が断行される」 — 1871 abolition of domains and prefectural administration.
- 国立国会図書館「第1章 立憲国家への始動」 — Freedom and People's Rights movement and demands for a national assembly / constitution.
- 国立公文書館「大日本帝国憲法が発布される」 — 11 February 1889 promulgation.
- 国立公文書館「第1回帝国議会が開会する」 — November 1890 first Imperial Diet.

## Material corrections

Generation-stage wording was corrected where it went beyond the reopened evidence. In particular, the Qing-population claim was removed from the 18th-century Asia unit; the B(1) category list was aligned to MEXT's actual wording; source types were narrowed to the core A(2) examples; the world-market sentence no longer presents Asian products as a single causal “premise”; and the B(4) contemporary-issues unit now follows MEXT's explicit paired viewpoints instead of an unsupported grab-bag of examples.
