## Independent evidence review

The current MEXT high-school Geography/History commentary was reopened for 日本史探究 A. Generated wording and generated formal placement were treated as untrusted.

MEXT places A(1)「黎明期の日本列島と歴史的環境」 around the transition from Paleolithic to Jomon culture and the establishment of Yayoi culture. It explicitly asks learners to consider natural environment and human life, relations with the Asian/Pacific region, and the transition from hunting-gathering to farming society.

A(2)「歴史資料と原始・古代の展望」 develops source skills and hypothesis formation. MEXT explicitly gives a Chinese-history + archaeology example for investigating the formation of small polities and political integration in the Japanese archipelago.

A(3)「古代の国家・社会の展開と画期」 explicitly includes state formation and Kofun culture; learners compare the distribution, scale, form and grave goods of tumuli and consider broad political alliances and continental relations.

Specific life/culture claims were independently cross-checked with public institutional material from Tokyo National Museum, the National Museum of Japanese History, and the official Jomon archaeological-sites resource.

## Material corrections

- `paleolithic-environment`: changed from a chipped-stone-tool fact card into an A(1) environment/adaptation unit; added independently supported hunting/gathering and mobile-life context.
- `jomon-society`: retained pottery, hunting/gathering/fishing and settlement, but tied them to environmental adaptation and independently supported cooking/storage use.
- `yayoi-agriculture`: aligned to MEXT's water-rice-agriculture question and the transition to farming society; linked agriculture to social-structure change rather than presenting metal tools as an isolated fact.
- `yayoi-polities-exchange`: moved from the generated A(1) placement to A(2) and rewrote it as a source-combination case study. The generated Himiko memorization sentence was removed because the curricular objective here is to combine Chinese historical writings with archaeology to examine small-polity formation and political integration.
- `kofun-yamato`: moved from the generated A(1) placement to A(3). The unit now uses MEXT's formal state-formation/Kofun-culture framework and avoids reducing the topic to an undefined “Yamato government” statement.
- `ancient-sources-outlook`: strengthened the source-method focus: collect/read evidence, understand source characteristics and limitations, form a hypothesis, and carry it into A(3).

## Publication decision

All six units passed after correction and contain no images. They are learner-facing. In the public selector, the source-method A(2) unit is placed before the Kofun A(3) unit so learner-facing order follows the formal curriculum structure even though the generation backlog had a different order.
